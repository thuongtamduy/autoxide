# Using the Autoxide name

**English** · [Tiếng Việt](TRADEMARK.vi.md)

The **code** of Autoxide is free: you may use, modify and redistribute it under the MIT or Apache-2.0 license, at your
option ([`LICENSE-MIT`](LICENSE-MIT), [`LICENSE-APACHE`](LICENSE-APACHE)). Those licenses do not grant rights to the **name
and logo** "Autoxide". This page says how the name may be used so that people are never misled about what is official.
It is a plain-language policy of the project, not legal advice, and the project does not claim a registered trademark.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, module or service **works with**, **is built on** or **is compatible with** Autoxide, and
  link to the project.
- Name a module crate **`autoxide-<name>`** or `<name>-for-autoxide` and use the topic `autoxide-module`
  ([`docs/MODULE_ECOSYSTEM.md`](docs/MODULE_ECOSYSTEM.md)).
- Write about Autoxide, teach it, review it, and use its name in articles, talks, videos, tutorials and job posts.
- Make a **fork** and say it is a fork of Autoxide ("a fork of Autoxide", with a link), as long as the fork has
  its own name.
- Use the unmodified name in text to refer to the project, and the project's logo (if one exists) unmodified to link
  to it.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a product, company, domain, package or fork **"Autoxide"**, or something confusingly similar, as if it were the
  project (for example `Autoxide Cloud` offered as the project's own service).
- Say or suggest that your module, product or service is **official, certified or endorsed** by the Autoxide
  project when it is not. "Official module" means a module in the `modules/` directory of this repository.
- Use the name or logo in a way that implies the project's maintainers are affiliated with you, sponsor you or stand
  behind your work.
- Change the logo, or use it as the logo of your own product.

## Modules and distributions

- A community module may use the `autoxide-` prefix (see above) but must say in its README that it is **not an
  official module** unless it is listed in this repository.
- A hosted service built on Autoxide may say "powered by Autoxide". It must not be named in a way that makes users believe
  the Autoxide project operates it.
- A **modified distribution** (binaries or images you build from changed code) must not be called "Autoxide" without
  saying it is modified and by whom.

## Questions and requests

If you want to use the name in a way not covered here, or you see a use that misleads people, open an issue (or
contact the maintainers as in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md)). We would rather say yes to honest uses than have people guess.
