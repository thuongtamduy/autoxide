Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Customers

The customers module keeps a sales profile for every buyer of a shop, builds the purchase history from order events, and groups customers for price lists and promotions. It is registered after orders and works without it (no history, no groups in carts).

Profiles

  • One profile per buyer in a shop, keyed by the buyer account and/or the phone number. The phone is stored next to a keyed hash (HMAC with a per-shop key, the same buyer_key that orders writes), so the same number matches inside one shop and never across shops.
  • A profile is created on the buyer’s first order (orders.order_placed), when staff register a customer at the counter (POST /api/v1/customers), or on the buyer’s first GET /api/v1/customers/me.
  • A profile kept by phone joins a buyer account only when the account’s phone number is verified (one-time code, compliance). Registering a number at the counter does not give the history to whoever types that number later.
  • org_id is the unit where the customer registered or first bought. Staff with customers.read at unit / subtree scope see only the customers of their units; a tenant scope sees all.

Purchase history and totals

customers copies a summary of every order from the events of orders (it never reads the orders tables). GET /api/v1/customers/{id}/orders lists them.

  • Total spent = what the buyer paid for goods of completed orders, minus refunds: after all discounts (coupons and vouchers included), with tax, without shipping, without the part paid with points or gift cards. The definition is fixed (decision D-226).
  • Orders count = orders placed and not cancelled.
  • Events delivered twice never count twice.

Masked contact details

Every list and detail for staff (REST, MCP tools, exports) shows phone_masked (09•• ••• 412) and email_masked (n•••@gmail.com). To see them in full, call GET /api/v1/customers/{id}/contact?reason=… with permission customers.contact.reveal; every call emits customers.contact_revealed, which the audit log keeps (who, which customer, the reason). The buyer sees their own profile unmasked at GET /api/v1/customers/me.

Customer groups

  • Manual groups: staff add and remove members (POST / DELETE /api/v1/customers/groups/{id}/members/{profile_id}).

  • Rule groups: members follow a rule, all conditions must hold:

    {"total_spent_min": 5000000, "orders_count_min": 3, "last_order_within_days": 180,
 "tier_in": ["gold"], "tags_any": ["<tag id>"], "org_in": ["<unit id>"]}

    Members are updated shortly after a profile changes and again every night (rules about time, like “ordered in the last 180 days”, change without any event). GET /groups/{id}/preview counts the matching customers now.

  • A group of a unit only matches customers of that unit and below.

  • price_group (e.g. vip) is the customer group code that price lists of catalog use. When a buyer is in several groups with a code, the group first by name wins.

  • Carts of orders receive the buyer’s group ids (for promotions limited to groups), the membership tier and the price group.

Loyalty points and tiers

  • Rules (POST /api/v1/customers/loyalty/rules) are dated data: earning unit (earn_per_minor, e.g. 10,000 VND → points_per_unit), value of one point, validity in months, minimum to redeem, and the largest share of an order points may pay. A new version starts at effective_from (now or later) and never changes the past.
  • Points are earned when an order completes, on what the buyer paid for goods (definition above), times the tier multiplier. Each earning is a lot that expires at the end of the day (Vietnam time) points_valid_months later. Redeeming uses the lot that expires first.
  • Paying with points: send "tenders": [{"kind": "points", "input": {"points": 500}}] to POST /api/v1/orders/checkout. Points are held until the order is confirmed (then used) or cancelled / unpaid in time (then given back). The payment partner collects only the rest.
  • Tiers (POST /loyalty/tiers): the highest tier whose 12-month spending threshold is reached, checked when an order completes and every day. The tier code goes to carts (promotions by tier) and to group rules (tier_in).
  • Manual adjustments: POST /api/v1/customers/{id}/points/adjustments with customers.loyalty.adjust, a reference (never applied twice) and a reason.
  • Voucher rewards (POST /loyalty/voucher-rewards, kinds birthday, purchase, points) are granted through the promotions module; a birthday gives one voucher a year, an order one voucher, and an exchange for points takes the points first and gives them back if the voucher cannot be granted. Buyers see their points, the rules in force and the rewards at GET /me/points and GET /me/points/rewards, and exchange with POST /me/points/vouchers.
  • Ledger: unused points are a liability (loyalty.liability) at their value when earned; redeeming credits buyer.prepayments, expiry credits loyalty.breakage.

Wishlist, recently viewed, reminders, referrals

  • Wishlist (/me/wishlist): products or single variants, up to AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_WISHLIST_MAX. Right after signing in, send the list kept in the browser to POST /me/wishlist/merge; duplicates are skipped.
  • Recently viewed (/me/recently-viewed): signed-in buyers only (guests keep it in the browser), newest first, old entries removed every day.
  • Wishlist alerts (GET/PUT /me/wishlist/alerts): off until the buyer turns them on, with their consent (consent_text, consent_version, kept in the consent register under the purpose customers.wishlist_alerts). Two kinds: back in stock (needs inventory; skipped for products the buyer already asked inventory about) and price drop: at least AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_WISHLIST_PRICE_DROP_MIN_BP (default 5%) below the price when the product was added, never against a crossed-out price; after an alert the alerted price is the new reference. Alerts are batched every hour, only in the allowed advertising hours: one message with up to 10 products, at most AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_WISHLIST_ALERTS_PER_DAY messages a day (default 1), each product at most once every 7 days for each kind. Withdrawing the consent stops them at once.
  • Abandoned cart reminders (POST /reminder-rules, customers.engagement.manage): reminders are advertising. They go only to buyers who consented to marketing messages (consent register of compliance), consent is checked again right before sending, and they stop when the buyer orders or withdraws consent. notifications applies the allowed hours and the unsubscribe link.
  • Referrals: every buyer has a code (GET /me/referral); a new buyer enters it within AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_REFERRAL_APPLY_DAYS days and before the first order (POST /me/referral/apply). When that first order completes with enough goods, both are rewarded once, with points and optional vouchers (POST /referral-rules). The same phone or email as the referrer is refused.

Gift cards and store credit (off by default)

Prepaid value can fall under payment rules (Decree 52/2024/ND-CP in Vietnam). Both layers must be on before anything works: AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_STORED_VALUE_ENABLED=true (only after a qualified person checked the rules for your shop) and the plan features customers.gift_cards / customers.store_credit. Otherwise every endpoint below answers 403 customers.stored_value_disabled, and checkout does not offer them. autoxide doctor warns while they are on.

  • Value is spent only on orders of the shop that issued it. There is no way to withdraw, cash out, top up with money or move value to another person.
  • Gift cards (customers.stored_value.manage): POST /gift-cards issues a card worth at most AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_GIFT_CARD_MAX_MINOR; the 16-character code is in that answer only (the shop keeps a hash and the last 4 characters). The same reference never issues a second card. POST /gift-cards/lookup finds a card by code; POST /gift-cards/{id}/void ends a lost card.
  • Wrong codes are counted per person: after AUTOXIDE_CUSTOMERS_GIFT_CARD_LOOKUPS_PER_HOUR wrong codes in an hour, lookups answer 429 customers.lookup_throttled for the rest of the hour. Correct codes are not counted.
  • Store credit comes only from staff compensation: POST /{id}/store-credit/adjustments with a reference and a reason (the same reference applies once; it never goes below zero).
  • Buyers: GET /me/gift-cards, POST /me/gift-cards/attach (with the code), GET /me/store-credit. At checkout, pay part or all of an order with the tenders gift_card ({"code": "…"}) or store_credit ({"amount": <at most>}, optional); a signed-in account is required. The value is held until the order is confirmed and comes back if the order is cancelled or the hold runs out.
  • The ledger shows what is still owed on cards and store credit (gift_card.liability, store_credit.liability).

Tags, notes, custom fields

Profiles are records of kind customers.customer: internal tags, notes and custom fields come from the shared kernel endpoints (/api/v1/kernel/tags, record notes, custom field definitions with target customers.customer). Filter the list by tags with ?tags=<id>,<id>.

Permissions

PermissionAllows
customers.readSee profiles in scope (masked), purchase history, groups
customers.manageRegister at the counter, edit profiles, tag profiles
customers.contact.revealSee full phone and email (audited)
customers.segments.manageCreate, change, delete groups and their members
customers.loyalty.manageLoyalty rules, tiers and voucher rewards (whole shop)
customers.loyalty.adjustAdd or remove points by hand
customers.engagement.manageCart reminders and referral rewards
customers.stored_value.manageIssue, look up and void gift cards; change store credit

Buyers need no permission: /me only needs a signed-in account.

Personal data

The profile, purchase history and group memberships are part of the user’s data export. When an account is deleted, the profile loses every personal field (name, phone, email, birthday, custom fields) and its link to the account; totals and order summaries stay without anything that identifies the person. Legal basis to check with a qualified person: Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025.
