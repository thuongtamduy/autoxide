Shipping (shipping module)

Tiếng Việt

The shipping module keeps buyers’ address books, computes shipping rates set by each shop, plugs in shipping carriers as connector modules, follows shipments and reconciles cash on delivery (COD). It does not depend on orders or catalog: orders quote through autoxide_shipping::api and send shipment requests as events.

Address book

Signed-in buyers keep up to AUTOXIDE_SHIPPING_MAX_ADDRESSES_PER_BUYER (default 20) addresses:

MethodPathWho
GET, POST/api/v1/shipping/me/addressesany signed-in account, own addresses only
GET, PATCH, DELETE/api/v1/shipping/me/addresses/{id}same
POST/api/v1/shipping/me/addresses/{id}/defaultsame
GET/api/v1/shipping/buyers/{buyer_id}/addressesshipping.addresses.read (customer care)
  • Vietnam: province_code and commune_code come from the official list of administrative units of the address module (two levels since 2025-07-01). The commune must belong to the province and be in force; the names are saved with the address, so a later change of the list does not change saved addresses.
  • Other countries: a generic form — province_code is the region (state, province…), city the city, postal_code optional. In responses the city is address.commune_name and address.commune_code is empty.
  • Exactly one address is the default (the first one automatically). Deleting the default makes the most recently changed other address the default.
  • An address book is personal data: it is part of the account’s data export and is erased when the account is deleted. Shipments keep their own copy of the recipient.

Other modules read it through dyn AddressBook (get, default_for).

Shipping rates

Each shop sets its own rates under /api/v1/shipping (permission shipping.settings.manage at the unit):

MethodPathWhat
GET, POST/zoneszones: groups of provinces (or whole countries)
GET, PATCH, DELETE/zones/{id}one zone (deleting it removes its tiers)
PUT/zones/{id}/regionsreplace the provinces of a zone
GET, POST/methodsshipping methods: table_rate, carrier, pickup
GET, PATCH, DELETE/methods/{id}one method with its tiers and pickup points
PUT/methods/{id}/ratesreplace the weight tiers of a method
PUT/methods/{id}/pickup-pointsreplace the stores of a pickup method
POST/quotequote a cart (shipping.read at the store) — the same computation orders use
  • Inheritance: a store uses the active methods of its own unit and of every unit above it; a method of a nearer unit replaces one with the same code higher up (a store can override the chain’s standard).
  • Zones: a zone listing the destination province wins over a zone for the whole country.
  • Weight: chargeable weight = max(actual weight, volumetric weight); volumetric weight of an item = length × width × height (mm³) / volumetric_divisor (default 6000) grams, rounded up. Items that do not need shipping (digital goods, services) weigh nothing; a cart with none to ship gets no options. An item without weight uses the method’s default_item_weight_grams; without one, the method is listed in unavailable with shipping.weight_missing.
  • Tiers: min_weight_grams ≤ weight < max_weight_grams (no maximum = no limit) and longest side ≤ max_side_mm; each step_grams (or part) above the minimum adds step_amount. Overlapping tiers are refused.
  • Free shipping: free_over compared with the order value after discounts; the option shows free_over so a cart can say how much more to buy.
  • VAT: fees are VAT-inclusive; each option carries the method’s tax_category so the order puts the VAT line of the shipping fee on the invoice.
  • Store pickup: one option per store, fee = the method’s handling_fee, no destination or weight needed.
  • Every option has an option_key. Send it back when placing the order: ShippingRates::confirm_option answers shipping.option_changed when the fee or destination changed.

Carrier accounts

/api/v1/shipping/carrier-accounts (permission shipping.carriers.manage, plan feature shipping.carriers): GET, POST, PATCH /{id}, POST /{id}/test. Credentials are stored encrypted per tenant and never shown again (only credential_fields). The create answer holds webhook_path once — give it to the carrier. check_status (never, ok, failed) is set by the test call and whenever the carrier refuses the credentials. A carrier method quotes through its account; a carrier that does not answer within AUTOXIDE_SHIPPING_CARRIER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS is listed in unavailable (shipping.carrier_unavailable) without breaking the quote.

Shipments

MethodPathPermission
GET/shipments?order_id&status&org_id&tracking, /shipments/{id}shipping.shipments.read
POST/shipments (by hand), /shipments/{id}/book, /cancel, /split, /statusshipping.shipments.manage
PUT/shipments/{id}/parcelsshipping.shipments.manage
GET/shipments/{id}/labelshipping.shipments.read
  • Orders ask for a shipment with the event orders.fulfillment_requested (returns: orders.return_pickup_requested), payload {"after": {"shipment": ShipmentRequest}}. The same order_id + request_key never creates a second shipment; the same key with other content is refused and logged. orders.order_cancelled cancels the shipments not picked up yet.
  • Statuses: pending, booking, ready_to_ship, picked_up, in_transit, out_for_delivery, delivered, delivery_failed, returning, returned, ready_for_pickup, picked_up_by_buyer, cancelled, lost. They only move forward along a fixed table (a new attempt after delivery_failed is allowed). Carrier updates that arrive late or out of order are kept in the tracking history but do not move the status back.
  • Booking calls the carrier outside any database transaction: pending → booking is saved first, then the carrier is called with the shipment id as client_reference. A shipment stuck in booking asks the carrier (find_by_reference) before ever booking again (every 5 minutes for shipments stuck over 10 minutes).
  • A method with auto_book books as soon as the order asks; otherwise staff check the goods and press book.
  • Parcels: one parcel at first; PUT /parcels splits the items into several parcels before booking. split moves some lines to a new shipment (partial delivery); cash on delivery stays with the original.
  • Events (after is a ShipmentEvent, without personal data): shipping.shipment_created, …_booked, …_status_changed, …_delivered, …_delivery_failed, …_returned, …_cancelled, shipping.booking_failed, shipping.cod_collected.

Carrier webhooks

POST /api/v1/shipping/webhooks/{carrier_code}/{hook}/{token} is public: hook names the tenant and token the carrier account, so the carrier sends no tenant header. This is the webhook_path of a new carrier account. The older address …/webhooks/{carrier_code}/{token} still works; there the tenant comes from the autoxide-tenant header or the domain. The connector checks the signature on the raw body; a wrong signature answers 401 and nothing is stored. A valid body is stored as received (secret headers removed) and answered 202; a job applies it. Repeated updates are applied once (event_key); bodies above AUTOXIDE_SHIPPING_WEBHOOK_MAX_BYTES answer 413. Carriers without webhooks are polled every 30 minutes.

Cash on delivery (COD)

The carrier collects the cash and pays it to the seller under its contract; Autoxide only records who owes what and reconciles (it never holds the money). With the ledger module installed:

  • a delivered COD shipment records “the carrier owes the amount” (clearing.carrier_cod) against the buyer’s receivable;
  • POST /api/v1/shipping/cod/statements (permission shipping.cod.manage) imports a carrier statement: CSV with the columns tracking_number, cod_amount, fee (minor units) or fetch: true for carriers with a statement API. Matching lines settle the shipment (money in transit + carrier fee against what the carrier owed; event shipping.cod_settled with seller_org_id). The same reference with the same content is not imported twice.
  • Lines that differ (amount, unknown tracking number, not delivered) and COD still missing from statements after AUTOXIDE_SHIPPING_COD_SETTLEMENT_DAYS become discrepancies (GET /cod/discrepancies, event shipping.cod_mismatch). Resolve one with a code of the reason list shipping.cod_resolution and a note; written_off writes the missing amount off. refresh-tracking asks the carrier for the latest tracking first. Statements and discrepancies can be read with shipping.cod.read (accounting, audit) or shipping.cod.manage.

Contract for other modules

#![allow(unused)]
fn main() {
let rates = context.require::<dyn autoxide_shipping::api::ShippingRates>()?;   // quote, confirm_option
let book = context.require::<dyn autoxide_shipping::api::AddressBook>()?;
let carriers = context.require::<dyn autoxide_shipping::api::CarrierRegistry>()?;
}

Register your module after shipping in crates/app/src/modules.rs. Build requests with the constructors (QuoteRequest::new, QuoteItem::new(…).weight(…), ShipmentRequest::new): the types are #[non_exhaustive] so new fields never break callers.

orders asks for a shipment by publishing orders.fulfillment_requested (or orders.return_pickup_requested) with {"after": {"shipment": <ShipmentRequest>}}; shipping answers with shipping.shipment_* events whose after is a ShipmentEvent (no personal data).

Writing a carrier connector

A connector is its own module (shipping-<carrier>) registered after shipping. It implements ShippingCarrier and registers it in Module::start:

#![allow(unused)]
fn main() {
fn start(&mut self, context: &mut StartContext<'_>) -> Result<(), Error> {
    let registry = context.require::<dyn autoxide_shipping::api::CarrierRegistry>()?;
    registry.register(Arc::new(MyCarrier::new()))
}
}

Rules every connector follows (see autoxide_shipping::api::fake::FakeCarrier for a complete example):

  • create_shipment must not create a second shipment for the same client_reference (the Autoxide shipment id); find_by_reference finds one created by a call whose answer was lost.
  • parse_webhook checks the signature on the raw body (constant-time comparison) before reading it, and returns shipping.webhook_signature when it is wrong.
  • Convert carrier times to UTC (Vietnamese carriers often send local time without a zone).
  • Map carrier statuses to the common ShipmentStatus; keep the carrier’s own text in carrier_status.
  • Never log credentials: CarrierCredentials hides its values from Debug.

AUTOXIDE_SHIPPING_FAKE_CARRIER=true registers the fake carrier fake for trying everything on a development machine. autoxide doctor warns while it is on; never turn it on in production.

Delivery dates

Delivery calendars live under /api/v1/shipping/calendars (shipping.settings.manage): a time zone (default Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh), working days of the week, and special days set with PUT /calendars/{id}/days/{YYYY-MM-DD} — closed (holiday, entered when it is announced; nothing is hard-coded) or special_hours with its own cut-off. A method links a calendar (calendar_id), a daily cut-off (cutoff_minute, 660 = 11:00) and same_day.

Every option of a quote carries eta: earliest_date and latest_date as calendar dates in the calendar’s time zone (working days after the dispatch day), and order_by, today’s cut-off when it is still ahead. Methods without a calendar use Monday to Saturday, Vietnam time. Store pickup options carry ready_at (order time + the pickup point’s prep_hours, moved to the next opening time of the store from its public profile, 08:00–21:00 when the store has none) and hold_days.

Public estimate for product pages

GET /api/v1/shipping/public/estimate?variant_id=…&quantity=1&province_code=79 needs no sign-in. It runs the same quote as checkout for a one-line cart to a province (fees, free-shipping threshold, eta with today’s cut-off) and answers estimate: true — the real fee is always computed again for the cart. The variant’s weight, size, selling unit and price come from the catalog module. Answers are cacheable for 60 seconds.

Automatic answers in support

When the support module is installed, shipping answers the question “where is my parcel?” (intent shipping.tracking, by tracking number or order number) with the real status of the shipment, in the buyer’s language. Only the owner gets an answer: the buyer account of the shipment, or a verified phone number equal to the recipient’s. When the shipment is not found, an order has several shipments, or the asker cannot be shown to be the owner, there is no automatic answer and staff reply.
