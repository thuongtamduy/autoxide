Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Newsletter

Tiếng Việt: NEWSLETTER.vi.md

The newsletter module keeps mailing lists and their subscribers, takes sign-ups from the public with a confirmation mail (double opt-in), unsubscribes in one click, and sends campaigns in batches at a rate you choose, without ever sending one person the same campaign twice. It keeps a suppression list fed by bounces and complaints, can turn each new post of the content module into a campaign, and exports or erases everything about an email address. It does not track opens or clicks. It is API only: the website shows the sign-up form, the confirmation page and the preferences page; the admin screens are a separate project. It lives under /api/v1/newsletter. The full contract is in openapi.json.

No third-party account is needed: spam is stopped by the core’s shared anti-spam layer, and mail goes through the mail module (your SMTP relay).

Before you send

  1. An SMTP relay that signs with DKIM. Gmail and Yahoo refuse bulk mail that is not authenticated, and the one-click unsubscribe headers (RFC 8058) only count when the DKIM signature covers them. Set AUTOXIDE_MAIL_SMTP_URL and AUTOXIDE_MAIL_FROM to a relay that signs your domain (SPF and DMARC too).
  2. AUTOXIDE_HTTP_PUBLIC_URL — the address of this API, e.g. https://api.example.com. Every campaign mail carries List-Unsubscribe: <https://api.example.com/api/v1/newsletter/public/unsubscribe?token=…>.
  3. AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_URL — your website’s confirmation page, with {token}. Set it even if no list uses double opt-in: suspicious sign-ups and people re-joining a list they left are confirmed by mail on every list.
  4. AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_PREFERENCES_URL — your website’s preferences page, with {token}; the footer of every campaign mail links there.
  5. AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_POSTAL_ADDRESS — the sender’s name and postal address, printed in the footer; required for advertising campaigns.

Each tenant can replace these for itself with tenant settings (PUT /api/v1/kernel/settings/{name}, see “Settings and state of each tenant” in DEPLOY.md): newsletter.link.confirm, newsletter.link.preferences (a page on one of the tenant’s own hosts) and newsletter.postal_address. When a tenant has not set its postal address, the owner’s name and address from its legal profile (module compliance) are used before the server’s.

Nothing here is needed to start the server: a feature that needs a missing setting answers 503 with a code naming it (public_url_missing, newsletter.confirm_url_missing, newsletter.preferences_url_missing, newsletter.postal_address_missing).

Permissions

PermissionAllows
newsletter.readReading lists, subscribers (personal data), campaigns with progress, suppressions
newsletter.manageLists, importing and changing subscribers, writing draft campaigns, suppressions, bounces
newsletter.sendSending, scheduling, pausing, resuming and cancelling campaigns; test mails; new_posts: send on a list
newsletter.exportExporting or erasing everything about an email address
newsletter.subscribeAdding sign-ups without the anti-spam checks for anonymous visitors (a website server’s own API key)

An agent with newsletter.manage but not newsletter.send can write campaigns that a person then reviews and sends: changing a scheduled campaign moves it back to draft, and setting a list to send new posts automatically needs newsletter.send, so nothing goes out that nobody with newsletter.send approved. Visitors need nothing: everything under /api/v1/newsletter/public/ is open. Give newsletter.subscribe only to the API key of a website server; being signed in is not enough to skip the anti-spam checks.

Lists

curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/newsletter/lists \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)" \
  -d '{"key": "weekly", "name": {"en": "Weekly news", "vi": "Tin hằng tuần"},
       "description": {"en": "Every Monday."}, "public": true}'
  • double_opt_in (default true): public sign-ups get a confirmation mail and receive nothing until they open its link.
  • public (default false): shown by GET /public/lists and open to public sign-up.
  • new_posts: off (default), draft or send — see “New posts”. Setting send needs newsletter.send.
  • PATCH and DELETE need If-Match. A list with an unfinished campaign cannot be deleted (409 newsletter.list_in_use); deleting a list deletes its subscriptions.

The website: sign-up

  1. GET /api/v1/newsletter/public/lists?locale=vi — the public lists and the token_scope (newsletter:subscribe).
  2. Each time the form is shown (never in cached HTML): GET /api/v1/antispam/token?scope=newsletter:subscribe — a form token. Show a hidden trap input that people leave empty, and a consent box that is not ticked in advance.
  3. POST /api/v1/newsletter/public/subscribe:
{"email": "ada@example.com", "lists": ["weekly"], "locale": "vi",
 "consent_text": "Gửi tôi bản tin hằng tuần.", "source": "footer",
 "form_token": "…", "trap": ""}
  • The answer is always 202 {"data": {"accepted": true}} — new address, waiting for confirmation, already subscribed, blocked, trap filled, too many requests: nobody can learn which addresses are on a list. Tell the visitor to check their inbox.
  • The request only checks the data and the anti-spam token, then queues the sign-up; a background job stores it and sends the confirmation mail a moment later. So the time the answer takes does not depend on the address either. The job worker must be running (it is, in the default all process role).
  • Wrong data: 422 with every problem in errors (a key that is not a public list is lists[0]). A missing or expired token: 400 antispam.token_* — reload the form.
  • The consent text, its version, the time, the source and a hash of the network block are kept with the subscription as proof.
  • The confirmation mail contains the list names and the link — never anything the visitor typed, so the form cannot be used to send spam to someone else. At most AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_MAILS_PER_DAY such mails go to one mailbox a day. Spellings that reach the same mailbox count together: letter case, a +tag after the name, and dots in a Gmail name.
  • The confirmation mail is always needed, even on a list without double opt-in, for a sign-up the anti-spam checks find suspicious (sent too fast…) and for an address that unsubscribed from that list or is still waiting for confirmation: nobody can sign someone else back up.
  • Every stored sign-up request counts towards AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_MAILS_PER_DAY for its address, whether it needs a mail or not, so answers take about as long either way. If the mail cannot be queued (for example AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_URL is not set and the list has no double opt-in), the answer is still 202 and the reason goes to the server log.
  • A website server may call with its own API key instead, if that key has the permission newsletter.subscribe: no token needed, limited to AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_SUBSCRIBE_PER_USER_PER_HOUR. Any other signed-in caller (for example a self-registered account) goes through the same checks as an anonymous visitor: without a form token it gets 400 antispam.token_missing.

The website: confirmation and preferences pages

  • Confirmation. The mail links to AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_URL. The page sends POST /api/v1/newsletter/public/confirm {"token": "…"} (opening the link alone, as mail scanners do, changes nothing). 200 lists the confirmed lists; 400 newsletter.confirmation_invalid means the link expired (after AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_CONFIRM_HOURS) or was used.
  • Preferences. Every campaign mail links to AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_PREFERENCES_URL. The page reads GET /api/v1/newsletter/public/preferences?token=… (the address, name, language, and every public list and list the person is on) and saves with PUT /api/v1/newsletter/public/preferences?token=… {"lists": [{"key": "weekly", "subscribed": false}], "unsubscribe_all": false}. Unsubscribing takes effect at once; joining a public list from here is confirmed at once (the token came to the person’s inbox).
  • One click. Mail apps call POST /api/v1/newsletter/public/unsubscribe?token=… with the form body List-Unsubscribe=One-Click; the person leaves the list of that mail at once. The answer is the same for any token.

Unsubscribe links never expire. Each mail has its own random token; only its hash is stored. On a server with several tenants, a token may start with t<tenant id>.: it names the tenant itself, so confirm, preferences and unsubscribe calls work on any host of the API without the Autoxide-Tenant header. Send tokens back exactly as received (they may contain a .).

Campaigns

# Draft (newsletter.manage)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/newsletter/campaigns \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"list_id": "…", "subject": "This week", "body_text": "Hello…",
       "body_html": "<p>Hello…</p>", "locale": "en"}'
# Read it, then send exactly that version (newsletter.send)
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/api/v1/newsletter/campaigns/$ID/send \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'If-Match: "1762934400000000"'
  • POST /campaigns/{id}/test {"email": …} sends one test mail. GET /campaigns/{id}/preview gives the mail as it will be sent (subject label, footer), without sending anything.
  • GET /settings gives the settings in use (advertising label and daily limit, postal address) and, in missing, the settings still to set before sending.
  • send takes every confirmed subscriber of the list at that moment (the snapshot), then hands mails to the mail queue in batches of AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_BATCH_SIZE, at most AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_SEND_RATE_PER_MINUTE a minute. Right before each mail, suppressed, blocked and since-unsubscribed people are skipped.
  • schedule {"at": "2026-12-01T08:00:00Z"}, pause, resume, cancel. Pausing stops after the batch being sent; resuming sends to the recipients left — nobody gets a campaign twice, even when jobs are retried or run on several servers. Cancelling skips the mails not sent yet.
  • send and schedule need If-Match, so that the version you reviewed is the one sent. Only a draft or scheduled campaign can be changed; only one that never sent can be deleted.
  • Changing a scheduled campaign (PATCH, with any real change) moves it back to draft and drops its schedule; the jobs already waiting for the old schedule do nothing. Someone with newsletter.send must schedule or send it again.
  • body_html is stored and sent as is — it is not sanitised. An admin interface must never insert it into its own page: preview it only inside a sandboxed <iframe sandbox> (no scripts, no same origin).
  • GET /campaigns/{id} shows progress: total, pending, sent (handed to the mail queue), failed, skipped, plus bounced (hard bounces, also in failed) and unsubscribed (recipients who unsubscribed with the link of this campaign’s mail).
  • Every mail gets a footer (in the campaign’s locale) with the preferences link and the postal address, and the headers List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click.
  • One gap cannot be closed: if a server dies right after handing a mail to the queue and before recording it, that recipient is marked failed (interrupted) rather than risk a second mail.

Personalised campaigns (format: "template")

A campaign is format: "static" by default (sent as written). With "format": "template" the subject, body_text and body_html are templates of the shared template engine, rendered for each recipient: {{ subscriber.name }}, {{ subscriber.email }} (masked, e.g. a***@example.com), {{ list.name }}, {{ unsubscribe_url }} (the preferences page of this recipient), with {% if %}, {% for %} and the filters money, number, date. Nothing else is available. The templates are checked when the campaign is saved (syntax and variables, every problem at once with line and column); values put into body_html are always HTML-escaped. Preview and test mails use a sample recipient (Subscriber, s***@example.invalid).

Consent ledger

When the compliance module is installed, subscriptions share its consent ledger (docs/COMPLIANCE.md): every confirmed subscription is a consent (subject: the email, purpose marketing, scope newsletter.list:<id>, channel email, with the consent text, version and source the module stored), and unsubscribing withdraws it. Withdrawing an advertising consent in compliance (for this list, or every list) unsubscribes the person here. Right before each batch the ledger is asked again: a person whose consent was withdrawn there is skipped (consent_withdrawn); if the ledger cannot answer, the batch waits and retries. Subscriptions confirmed before this version are written into the ledger by the hourly job newsletter.backfill_consents (running it again adds nothing).

Suppressions and bounces

  • A suppressed address gets no campaign mail, whatever its subscriptions.
  • A permanent SMTP failure of a mail sent by this server that blames the address itself (mail.delivery_failed: enhanced status 5.1.x or 5.2.x, such as “no such mailbox”) suppresses the address (hard_bounce) and marks that mail failed. Other permanent errors (wrong SMTP password, sender not allowed, sending quota) are the sender’s problem: the mail is retried, then lands in the dead-letter queue (autoxide jobs failed), and no subscriber is blocked.
  • Mail services that report bounces call POST /api/v1/newsletter/bounces {"email": …, "type": "hard" | "soft" | "complaint", "reason": …} with an API key that has newsletter.manage. hard and complaint suppress at once; soft after AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_SOFT_BOUNCE_LIMIT reports.
  • GET/POST /suppressions, DELETE /suppressions/{id} manage the list by hand.

Subscribers

  • GET /subscribers?email=&list_id=&status=&blocked= — search by part of the address, list, subscription status, blocked or not.
  • POST /subscribers/import — up to 1000 people into lists as confirmed, with a required consent_source (where they agreed). Someone who unsubscribed from a list stays unsubscribed.
  • PATCH /subscribers/{id} (If-Match) — name, language, attributes, blocked, unsubscribe_list_ids. DELETE /subscribers/{id} deletes them for good.

New posts

A list with new_posts set to draft gets a draft campaign (title, summary and link of the post) each time the content module publishes a post (pages are left out); send sends it at once (setting send needs newsletter.send). new_post_locale keeps only posts in one language. The link is AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_SITE_URL followed by the post’s path; without it (or without the settings needed to send) campaigns stay drafts. A post makes at most one campaign per list, ever — republishing it makes nothing new. Importing many old posts publishes them all: keep new_posts at off or draft while you do.

Personal data

  • GET /api/v1/newsletter/data?email=… exports, and DELETE /api/v1/newsletter/data?email=… erases for good, everything about an address: subscriber, subscriptions with consent records, mails sent, confirmation links (permission newsletter.export). A suppression of the address is kept so that an address that bounced, complained or asked never to be mailed is not mailed again; delete it with DELETE /suppressions/{id} if the person asks.
  • Subscribers are not tied to user accounts: requests go by email. When an account is deleted, lists and campaigns it created stay; created_by becomes null.
  • Network addresses are never stored: only a keyed hash of the network block, inside the consent record.
  • Events (and so webhooks) carry ids and list keys only, never addresses or names.
  • A daily job deletes subscriptions left unconfirmed after AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_UNCONFIRMED_DAYS.

Legal

What the law asks of a newsletter depends on where you and your readers are. The module gives you settings, not rules written in the code; the texts below were not checked against the original legal texts — check them for your case before you rely on them.

  • Vietnam, Decree 91/2020/ND-CP (spam): advertising mail only to people who agreed before; a label at the start of the subject of advertising mail; at most 3 advertising mails to one address in 24 hours unless the person agreed otherwise; sender details and a way to refuse more. The module’s defaults follow this reading: mark a campaign advertising: true, and set AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_ADVERTISING_LABEL (default [QC]; some sources say [ADV]), AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_ADVERTISING_MAX_PER_DAY (default 3) and AUTOXIDE_NEWSLETTER_POSTAL_ADDRESS. Whether an editorial newsletter counts as advertising is for you to decide. A decree replacing 91/2020 was in draft at the time of writing.
  • Vietnam, Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025: consent must be freely given, clear and provable, silence is not consent, withdrawing must be as easy as giving, and people may ask for their data to be erased. The module keeps the consent text, version, time and source; double opt-in is on by default; unsubscribing is one click; export and erasure go by email. Check the deadlines for answering requests.
  • Elsewhere: for example the EU GDPR and ePrivacy rules, or the US CAN-SPAM Act (opt-outs within 10 business days — the module unsubscribes at once).
