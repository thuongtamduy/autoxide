Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Promotions

Tiếng Việt

The promotions module runs discounts as rules. A promotion reaches buyers in one of three ways: automatically, by coupon (a code anyone holding it can use) or as a voucher (a one-time right in one account’s wallet). Orders compute a cart, hold the usage of the promotions when the order is placed, confirm it when paid and release it when cancelled, through autoxide_promotions::api::Promotions. Everything is under /api/v1/promotions; every endpoint is described in docs/openapi.json, and the main operations are MCP tools (promotions.*).

Promotions

  • POST /promotions creates a draft. Every promotion has a start and a required end (ends_at). trigger is automatic, code or voucher; scope is items, order or shipping, which gives the stacking class product, order or shipping.
  • Kinds: percent_off, amount_off (per item for items, per order for order), fixed_price, buy_x_get_y (reward_selection: cheapest, same_item, specified; reward_value_bp 10000 = free), free_shipping, flash_sale (prices per variant with real units left), tiered (the highest tier reached), bundle_price (“3 items for 199,000”), add_on (a deal price for an extra item when a qualifying item is bought), gift_choice (the buyer picks gifts; free items come back as separate lines at price 0).
  • Conditions: targets (products, variants, categories with their subcategories), customer groups, channels, first order only, minimum order or quantity, and extra conditions on payment method, carrier, province, membership tier, client (web, app, pos), seller, new / returning customers, items already discounted, excluded targets; time windows per weekday in the store’s time zone.
  • Targets, flash-sale prices and translations have their own endpoints (PUT /promotions/{id}/targets, /flash-items, /translations/{locale}).
  • After a promotion is turned on, only a later end, more uses / budget / vouchers and its texts can change (promotions.locked_after_start); every change is kept in GET /promotions/{id}/versions.

Turning on and the law

POST /promotions/{id}/activation-check lists the checks without changing anything; POST /promotions/{id}/activate runs the same checks and fails with every failed check in errors:

  • the discount limit in force (50% of the price just before the promotion in Viet Nam, unlimited for a national promotion programme), using the catalog’s price history. Decree 239/2026/ND-CP (26 June 2026) repealed the limits of Decree 81/2018/ND-CP Articles 6 and 7 and left them to the Ministry of Industry and Trade; the circular is not found yet, so pack vn version 2 keeps 50% as a cautious default with the new basis, and keeps the 120 discount days a year that one summary says the decree dropped — check the original texts;
  • discount days per programme and per year;
  • the promotion notice recorded for each authority (PATCH /promotions/{id}/notices/{authority}; the province comes from the unit setting promotions.notice_province);
  • targets, and the catalog’s price floors (block stops, warn warns); conflicts are warnings.

Limits and notice rules are data with effective dates, not code: load them with autoxide task promotions.load_legal_data (the pack vn ships with the server, with its legal basis and “to be checked” notes) and read them at GET /legal-limits?at= and GET /notice-rules?at=. Without data in force a promotion cannot be turned on (promotions.no_legal_data). The pack was compiled from secondary sources: have it checked by a legal professional before real use.

To prepare the notices, GET /promotions/{id}/notice-export?format=json|csv (permission promotions.compliance and promotions.read; MCP tool promotions.export_notice) gives one entry per authority: whether a notice is required under the rules in force on the start day, the last day to submit, the legal basis and what was recorded. People submit the notice to the authority; Autoxide only prepares the data and records it.

A coupon, step by step

With API, TOKEN and ORG (your unit) as in the shop guide. The paths in this page are under /api/v1/promotions, so a promotion is at /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}.

P="$API/api/v1/promotions/promotions"
# 1. The province that receives your notices: a setting of your unit (units below it inherit it).
curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/organizations/units/$ORG/settings/promotions.notice_province" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"value": "01"}'

# 2. A draft: 10% off with a code, from now for 30 days.
PROMO=$(curl -sS "$P" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"trigger\": \"code\", \"kind\": \"percent_off\", \"value_bp\": 1000,
       \"currency\": \"VND\", \"locale\": \"vi\", \"title\": \"Giảm 10%\", \"terms\": \"Nhập mã GIAM10\",
       \"starts_at\": \"$(jq -rn 'now | todate')\", \"ends_at\": \"$(jq -rn 'now + 30 * 86400 | todate')\"}" \
  | jq -r .data.id)

# 3. The code buyers type (`"count": 100` instead of `code` generates codes).
curl -sS "$P/$PROMO/codes" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"purpose": "redeem", "code": "GIAM10"}'

# 4. What is still missing: a `promotions.notice_required` check names its authority, e.g. `province:01`.
curl -sS -X POST "$P/$PROMO/activation-check" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" | jq '.data.checks'

# 5. Record the notice you submitted to that authority, then turn the promotion on.
curl -sS -X PATCH "$P/$PROMO/notices/province:01" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"status": "submitted", "reference": "123/TB-SCT"}'
curl -sS -X POST "$P/$PROMO/activate" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
  • purpose is redeem for the coupon of a code promotion and claim for the claim code of a voucher promotion. A code taken by another promotion is refused (promotions.code_taken).
  • The setting holds the province code (GET /api/v1/address/provinces); without it the authority is province:unknown. {"status": "not_required"} records that no notice is needed.
  • Prices must exist before the promotion starts. The legal limit is computed from the price just before starts_at, and a price counts only from its valid_from (now, when you leave it out). A promotion that starts before the product’s first price gives that product no discount (withheld, reason promotions.reason.no_reference_price). Start the promotion after the price was set, or give the price an earlier valid_from when you add it (POST /api/v1/catalog/price-lists/{id}/prices).

Computing a cart

POST /evaluate (and Promotions::evaluate) computes a cart without holding anything:

  1. candidates: running, in their window, of the store or its parents (a seller’s promotions only on the seller’s items), conditions met; coupons and vouchers open their promotions — a wrong code or voucher comes back in rejected_codes with a reason, the cart still works;
  2. the product class, then order on what is left, then shipping. Two promotions stack only when both allow it (exclusivity, combines_with); stop_after stops lower priorities in the class. The unit setting promotions.stacking_mode picks priority (default, predictable) or best_for_buyer;
  3. the legal limit per line adds every discount (all classes, platform and seller vouchers together); the later discount is cut first (capped_by_law), then the price floor (capped_by_floor). A product with no price before the promotion gets no discount (withheld);
  4. each discount is shared among its payers (funding: platform, seller, brand, payment partner) to the cent; reasons are translated to the cart’s locale.

Percentages are rounded half up on the line total; order discounts are shared to lines in proportion to their amounts. The same cart always gives the same result and fingerprint.

A website menu can point to a promotion: the type promotions.promotion in the resource directory is public only while a public promotion runs, with its title, conditions and the standard path /promotions/<id>.

Sellers in platform promotions

On a marketplace, a seller joins a platform flash sale that has not started: POST /promotions/{id}/participations (permission promotions.participate on the seller’s unit, below the unit running the promotion) with the seller’s own products, each with a sale price or a discount and the units offered. The platform approves or rejects it (POST …/participations/{participation_id}/approve or /reject, permission promotions.publish); approved products join the flash sale and their discount is paid by the seller (funding). GET …/participations shows every application to the platform and their own to sellers.

Planning

All read-only, permission promotions.read:

  • POST /promotions/{id}/simulate {"carts": [...]} (1 to 20 carts, MCP tool promotions.simulate): each cart computed with the promotion (a draft works too) and without it, and difference_minor;
  • GET /promotions/{id}/conflicts: other promotions of the unit at the same time, on shared targets and in the same class, where one side is exclusive (only one of them applies to a line);
  • GET /promotions/calendar?from=&to=&org_id=: promotions running on those days (Vietnam dates, up to 366);
  • GET /promotions/{id}/stats?from=&to=: confirmed uses, discount, order value and who paid, per day.

Holding usage

Promotions::reserve recomputes the cart and holds, in one transaction, every counter: uses of the promotion and of the code, uses per buyer key (account, hashed phone, hashed address — a new account with the same phone gets no extra use), uses per day, budget, flash-sale units, gifts and vouchers. All or nothing; the same order and cart again returns the same hold. A different result from the one the buyer saw fails with promotions.changed. confirm makes it final; release gives the usage back (after confirmation only for promotions with return_usage_on_cancel; vouchers return to the wallet while valid). Holds not confirmed expire (promotions.expire_holds, every minute).

Vouchers

Buyers save the voucher of a claimable promotion (POST /public/promotions/{id}/claim), enter a claim code (POST /me/vouchers/claim-code, tries are limited) or receive one through grant_voucher (loyalty, birthday, compensation…; the same source + source_ref never gives twice) or by hand with a reason (POST /promotions/{id}/vouchers). GET /me/vouchers lists the wallet. The wallet is in the personal data export; deleting an account revokes unused vouchers.

On the website

GET /public/promotions?locale=&site= (no sign-in) lists the promotions marked public running now on a website: those of its unit and of the units above it, with their full conditions, the notices recorded with the Departments of Industry and Trade (authority and reference) and the largest discount the law in force today allows (legal_limit, from the law data). Pages with limit / cursor; send the ETag back in If-None-Match to get 304.

GET /public/promotions/for-product/{product_id}?variant_id=&site= lists the public coupons that lower the price of one variant, cheapest first: the price of one unit with the coupon, its conditions and the uses left (only when there is a real limit). The listed price stays the price without a coupon — show “X with code …”, not a discounted price. For a cart, Promotions::eligible (served by orders) gives the coupons and vouchers that work, those that do not yet with a reason (“add X to use”), and the best selection, so a picker can replace the code box.

GET /public/promotions/flash-sales?site= lists the public flash sales running now or starting within a day, with their products, sale prices and the units left at the sale price (a real count: held and sold units are taken off). A signed-in buyer can ask to be reminded before one starts (POST /public/promotions/{id}/reminders, permission promotions.use; DELETE cancels): one reminder is sent through notifications ten minutes before the start. Asking is recorded in the consent ledger as consent to that one reminder, not to advertising.

Returns

api::returns has the rules for orders: the refund of a line is the amount paid × returned / bought, the last unit taking the remainder; order discounts are taken back only when the unit setting promotions.clawback_on_return is proportional; gifts follow the promotion’s gift_return_policy.
