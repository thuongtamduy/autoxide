Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Money, ledger and accounting documents

Tiếng Việt

Three modules handle money in Autoxide:

ModuleWhat it doesOther modules use
taxtax categories, rates with effective dates, tax profiles, the Vietnam data pack (TAX.md)dyn TaxEngine
ledgerdouble-entry books, payments through licensed partners, money-flow modes, holds, payouts, VietQR transfers, exchange ratesdyn Ledger
invoicesdocument series, gapless numbering, e-invoices, print templates, accounting exports, archivesdyn Invoices

Autoxide never holds money itself. There is no wallet and no balance “of the buyer” kept by the platform: money goes from the buyer to a licensed payment partner or straight to the seller’s bank account. The ledger only records obligations (what is owed to whom) and checks them against the partners’ statements.

Legal notes in this guide (accounting law, e-invoices, withholding, data protection) are a starting point, not legal advice: check them against the original documents with someone qualified before running a real business on them.

All routes are under /api/v1/<module>, described in docs/openapi.json (served at /api/v1/openapi.json). Amounts are {"amount": "1999", "currency": "USD"} — an integer of the currency’s smallest unit, as a string. Rates and quantities are decimal strings such as "25345.5", never JSON numbers.

The ledger

  • One book per organization unit (the tenant’s root, a subsidiary, a seller that keeps its own accounts), in a book currency (default: the unit’s currency, else VND).
  • Accounts are named by purpose: cash.bank, receivable.customer, revenue.sales, tax.vat_output, payable.seller_held… plus a counterparty (org, customer, carrier, partner, supplier) when needed. Other modules add purposes with their own prefix.
  • Entries are double-entry and append-only: debits equal credits, every line is positive, all lines in one currency. A mistake is corrected with a reversing entry (POST /ledger/entries/{id}/reverse); an entry can be reversed once.
  • Every write takes a reference: sending the same reference with the same content returns the first result (200); with other content it is refused (reference_reused).
  • Monthly periods can be closed (POST /ledger/periods/{id}/close); a closed period takes no entry — post an adjustment in an open period. Closed periods do not reopen.
  • Tamper evidence: entries are sealed into a hash chain within seconds; autoxide doctor reports the first entry changed or removed outside the module (ledger.chain) and any balance that does not match its lines (ledger.balances).

Reading: GET /ledger/entries (filter by source_module and source_id to see the entries of a document), GET /ledger/accounts/{id}/balance?as_of=, GET /ledger/trial-balance.

Payments, flow modes, holds and payouts

A payment is created with POST /ledger/payments and paid through a payment partner (a connector module that implements the kernel trait PaymentGateway). The partner’s webhook (POST /ledger/webhooks/{provider}, signature checked on the raw body) marks it paid; a daily reconciliation compares the ledger with the partner’s statement and puts every difference in a queue for a person (GET /ledger/exceptions).

Each unit has a money-flow mode (PUT /ledger/flow-settings/{org_id}, inherited down the tree):

ModeWho receives the moneyWhat the ledger records
self_collect (default)the seller, directlythe sale and VAT in the seller’s book
partner_collecta licensed partner collects for the marketplacewhat is owed to the seller (payable.seller_held), held until the return period ends, then available for payout
split_at_gatewaythe partner splits the payment at onceonly the marketplace’s share

Holds are released by a job (every 5 minutes) or by hand (POST /ledger/holds/{id}/release). Payouts (POST /ledger/payouts) go through a PayoutGateway partner and never exceed the available balance, even when sent at the same time. Platform fees carry their own VAT; withholding for household businesses (Decree 117/2025/ND-CP — needs checking) only happens in the collect modes; core computes and books it, but does not yet withhold on each payout by itself, issue the withholding certificate or prepare the declaration (with the marketplace module, after 1.0 — see TAX.md).

VietQR bank transfers

POST /ledger/payments/vietqr returns a VietQR payload (NAPAS / EMVCo format) whose transfer note is a short code. Money goes straight to the unit’s bank account. A bank feed (BankFeed: a bank or a statement-reading service, by webhook or by polling) reports incoming transfers; a transfer with the right code and amount marks the payment paid, anything else (wrong amount, no code, paid twice, late) goes to the exceptions queue. Only a fake bank feed ships today: a real connector needs a contract with the bank or service.

Accounting documents

invoices keeps documents of these kinds: vat_invoice, sales_invoice, cash_register_invoice, retail_receipt, delivery_note, goods_receipt, cash_receipt, cash_payment, withholding_certificate.

  • A draft has no number; taxes are computed from the tax categories of its lines.
  • Issuing (POST /invoices/documents/{id}/issue) gives the next number of the series with no gaps, even under concurrent issues; issuing again returns the same number. Sales documents are booked in the seller’s ledger (receivable, revenue, output VAT).
  • E-invoices go to a provider (EInvoiceProvider, including invoices from cash registers) in a background job; a refused invoice is fixed and sent again with the same number. Issued documents are corrected only by an adjustment or a replacement, or cancelled. An e-invoice already sent to the tax authority cannot be cancelled where the seller’s tax jurisdiction forbids it (Vietnam since 2025-06-01, Decree 70/2025/ND-CP; tax pack rule einvoice.cancel_after_submission): 409 invoices.einvoice_not_cancellable with the legal basis — issue an adjustment (direction: decrease) or a replacement. One the tax authority rejected is cancelled locally. Autoxide does not send the error notice form 04/SS; lodge it at the provider’s or tax authority’s portal when it is required.
  • A unit may issue for a seller under a mandate (/invoices/mandates).

Exchange rates and foreign-currency documents

Rates live in the ledger, per tenant, per currency pair, with an effective date: a rate is used from its day until a later rate of the same pair.

  • Record by hand: POST /ledger/exchange-rates {"base": "USD", "quote": "VND", "rate": "25345.5", "effective_date": "2026-11-17"} (permission ledger.settings_manage for the whole tenant). Recorded rates are never changed; a different rate for the same pair and day is refused.
  • Fetch from a rate source: POST /ledger/exchange-rates/fetch (a connector implementing the kernel trait ExchangeRateSource; only a fake source ships today).
  • Look up: GET /ledger/exchange-rates/effective?base=USD&quote=VND&on=2026-11-17 — the latest rate on or before that day; on the same day a hand-entered rate wins over a fetched one. MCP tool ledger.exchange_rate.

A foreign-currency document takes the rate of its issue date when it is issued and keeps a copy (exchange_rate, exchange_rate_id, base_total); later rates never change it. Without a rate, issuing fails with invoices.rate_missing. Entries in a currency other than the book’s store the rate on every line and an amount in the book currency; the converted total is rounded once and shared between the lines, so the entry balances in both currencies. A reversal uses the rate of the entry it reverses. (Accounting Law 88/2015, Article 11: amounts in foreign currency are converted to đồng — needs checking.)

Printing documents

GET /invoices/documents/{id}/render?format=html|pdf&locale=vi|en prints a document.

  • HTML comes from a print template: the unit’s template for the document kind and language, else the tenant’s, else the built-in one. Templates use Jinja syntax ({{ buyer.name }}, {% for line in lines %}, filters money, number, date) and run in a sandbox: no files, no network, no other templates, limits on size (AUTOXIDE_TEMPLATE_MAX_BYTES), steps and output. Every value is HTML-escaped and there is no safe filter.
  • Manage templates with /invoices/templates (permission invoices.templates_manage). Saving checks the template and reports every problem at once with line and column: template.syntax_error, template.unknown_variable, template.required_variable_missing. GET /invoices/template-defaults gives the built-in template and the variables; POST /invoices/templates/preview tries a template without saving it.
  • PDF has a fixed A4 layout built from the same data (title, parties, lines with wrapping and page breaks, totals, signature blocks), with an embedded font that carries Vietnamese marks. Templates change the HTML only; for a PDF in your own layout, print the HTML from a browser.
  • Other modules (orders, inventory) print their delivery notes, goods receipts and receipts the same way: they create the document through dyn Invoices and call Invoices::render.

Accounting exports

POST /invoices/exports {"profile": "generic_journal", "format": "csv", "from": "2026-01-01", "to": "2026-01-31"} (permission invoices.export) makes a file in the background; follow the Location until status is ready, then GET /invoices/exports/{id}/download.

ProfileRows
generic_journalone row per entry line: posting date, entry, document date and number, description, account, debit, credit, currency, rate, amounts in the book currency, counterparty
vn_double_entrydebit/credit pairs (TK Nợ, TK Có, Số tiền) — the shape Vietnamese accounting software imports as general vouchers through its column-mapping step
generic_documentsone row per document line: date, series, number, buyer, item, quantity, price, VAT rate and amounts
  • CSV is UTF-8 with a BOM (opens in Excel with Vietnamese marks), cells starting with = + - @ are prefixed with ' (no formula injection). XLSX is also available.
  • Account numbers: the account’s own external_code, else the Vietnamese chart of accounts mapping of its purpose (modules/ledger/data/vn-accounts.json, e.g. revenue.sales → 5111 — needs checking by an accountant against Circulars 99/2025/TT-BTC and 133/2016/TT-BTC).
  • The vendor templates of MISA, Fast and other packages are downloaded inside each product and change between versions, so no vendor-specific profile ships yet; map the columns of vn_double_entry in the product’s import screen.

Keeping records for 10 years

Accounting documents and books are kept at least 10 years after the end of the accounting year (Accounting Law 88/2015/QH13 Article 41; Decree 174/2016/ND-CP Article 12 — needs checking).

  • Issued documents cannot be deleted (invoices.retention_hold); each carries retention_until.
  • Deleting a tenant for good keeps the accounting tables (ledger_*, invoices_series, invoices_documents, invoices_lines, invoices_mandates, invoices_archives); everything else is removed.
  • Archive copy: POST /invoices/archives (permission invoices.archive) writes the general journal (ledger/journal.csv), every document (documents.jsonl) and manifest.json (SHA-256 of every file) to the shared file storage under archives/<tenant>/<id>/ — outside the tenant’s folder, so it stays after the tenant is deleted. A deleted tenant without an archive gets one automatically.
  • After the retention period a daily job (invoices.purge_expired) removes the personal data of consumer buyers from expired documents (name, email, address, personal id); for deleted tenants it deletes expired documents and drafts. Documents still inside their retention period are never touched. The ledger of a deleted tenant is kept whole: its hash chain cannot be cut in part.

Writing a partner connector

A connector is a module of its own that depends only on the kernel and registers before ledger/invoices: it implements one of the kernel traits and contributes it in start with context.contribute::<dyn PaymentGateway>(Arc::new(…)) (or PayoutGateway, BankFeed, ExchangeRateSource, EInvoiceProvider). Every call receives the tenant: read that tenant’s credentials from the tenant secret store, never from environment variables. Check webhook signatures on the raw body with a constant-time comparison. Test with the fakes of autoxide-testkit (FakePaymentGateway, FakePayoutGateway, FakeBankFeed, FakeExchangeRateSource, FakeEInvoiceProvider).
