  • GET /api/v1/promotions/legal-limits: List the legal promotion limits in force on a day.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/me/vouchers: List the vouchers in your wallet, soonest to expire first.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/me/vouchers/claim-code: Enter a claim code to put its voucher into your wallet.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/notice-rules: List the promotion notice rules in force on a day.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions: List the promotions in your scope.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions: Create a promotion as a draft.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/calendar: List the promotions running on some days (calendar).
  • DELETE /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}: Delete a draft promotion.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}: Get a promotion with its rule, texts, targets and counters.
  • PATCH /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}: Change a promotion.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/activate: Turn a draft promotion on.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/activation-check: Check a promotion before turning it on, without changing anything.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/cancel: Cancel a promotion.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/codes: List the codes of a promotion.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/codes: Add codes to a promotion: one code you choose, or a batch of random codes.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/codes/export: Download every code of a promotion as CSV.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/conflicts: List the promotions that may clash with this one.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/end: End a promotion now.
  • PUT /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/flash-items: Replace the flash-sale prices of a draft promotion.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/notice-export: Export the data of a promotion notice, one entry per authority.
  • PATCH /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/notices/{authority_code}: Record that a promotion notice was submitted to an authority.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/participations: List the applications to a platform promotion.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/participations: Apply to join a platform flash sale with your products.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/participations/{participation_id}/approve: Approve a seller’s application.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/participations/{participation_id}/reject: Reject a seller’s application.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/pause: Pause a running or scheduled promotion.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/resume: Resume a paused promotion.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/simulate: Try a promotion on sample carts.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/stats: Get the daily counts of a promotion.
  • PUT /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/targets: Replace the targets (products, variants, categories) of a draft promotion.
  • PUT /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/translations/{locale}: Set the title and terms of a promotion in one language.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/versions: Get the audit trail of a promotion: one entry per change after it was turned on.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/promotions/{id}/vouchers: Put a voucher of a voucher promotion into a user’s wallet by hand.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions: List the promotions running now on this website (public).
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions/flash-sales: List the public flash sales running now or starting within a day (public).
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions/for-product/{product_id}: List the public coupons that lower the price of a product (public).
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions/{id}/claim: Save the voucher of a public promotion into your wallet (“Save” button).
  • DELETE /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions/{id}/reminders: Cancel your reminder of a flash sale.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/public/promotions/{id}/reminders: Ask to be reminded when a flash sale starts.
  • GET /api/v1/promotions/redemptions: List the promotion usage held for orders.
  • POST /api/v1/promotions/redemptions/{id}/release: Give back the promotion usage held for an order by hand (unstick it).
