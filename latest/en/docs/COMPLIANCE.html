Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Compliance (Vietnam)

Tiếng Việt

The compliance module helps a tenant run a commerce website in Vietnam without missing the basic legal duties. It is always on (it is not sold by plan). Every legal point that changes over time — lists of documents, deadlines, which logo domain is accepted — is data with an effective date (rules in modules/compliance/rules/vn.json, imported into the database), not code.

Not legal advice. The rules, checklists and templates shipped here were written from secondary sources. Every item says needs_legal_review: true: check it against the original legal text, with a legal professional, before you rely on it.

This guide explains the Ministry of Industry and Trade procedure with the mandatory page templates of content, then personal data (with the 72-hour breach runbook), reports of infringements with consumer complaints, and tracking tags with cookie consent.

The Ministry of Industry and Trade procedure

Vietnamese law (from 1 July 2026 the E-commerce Law 122/2025/QH15 and Decree 248/2026/ND-CP, which replaces Decrees 52/2013/ND-CP and 85/2021/ND-CP; before that, those two decrees — check the originals) requires the items below. The rules moit.checklist.* version 2 apply from 1 July 2026. A website notified or registered before then files again under the new decree within the transition period (one summary says 30 June 2027 — not confirmed); electronic identity checks of sellers and livestreamers apply from 1 January 2027. Which authority receives the files, whether online.gov.vn and the logos stay, is not confirmed yet — check.

  • a sales website (you sell your own goods) to be notified to the ministry — it then shows a blue “Đã thông báo Bộ Công Thương” logo;
  • a marketplace or other intermediary (others sell through you) to be registered — red “Đã đăng ký Bộ Công Thương” logo; the file includes a service project plan and operating regulations;
  • the website to show the owner’s information and its policies (general conditions, shipping, payment, returns, inspection, personal data protection, complaints).

A person submits on online.gov.vn; there is no public API. Autoxide keeps the data, tells you what is missing, drafts the dossier and shows the logo once you record the result.

1. Legal profile of the owner

One profile per organization unit. The tenant’s own profile belongs to the root unit (leave org_id out); a seller on a marketplace gets a profile on its unit. A website uses the profile of its own unit, or of the nearest unit above that has one.

POST /api/v1/compliance/legal-profiles
{
  "entity_type": "organization",
  "legal_name": "Cong ty TNHH Vi Du",
  "tax_code": "0101234567",
  "registration_number": "0101234567",
  "registration_issued_on": "2020-01-02",
  "registration_issued_by": "Phong Dang ky kinh doanh Ha Noi",
  "address": {"country": "VN", "province_code": "01", "commune_code": "00004", "line": "12 Trang Tien"},
  "representative_name": "Nguyen Van A",
  "phone": "024 1234 5678",
  "email": "lienhe@shop.example"
}
  • entity_type: individual, household_business or organization.
  • The address is checked against the administrative units of the address module (import them first: autoxide task address.import).
  • PATCH /legal-profiles/{id} changes some fields ("" clears a text, null clears address); send If-Match with the ETag you read.
  • kyc_profile_id links the owner’s identity verification; a marketplace checklist needs it verified.

Permissions: compliance.legal.read, compliance.legal.manage. Both have a scope: a seller’s staff see and change only their unit’s profile and websites.

2. Website

POST /api/v1/compliance/websites
{"domain": "shop.example", "kind": "sales"}
  • kind: sales (notification) or platform (registration). Changing kind later starts the procedure again.
  • channel: website (default) or mobile_app.
  • org_id: the unit of the website (e.g. a seller’s own store); left out, the whole tenant.

3. Mandatory pages

Write each page in content — the templates in the next section give a starting text — publish it, then link it to the website:

PUT /api/v1/compliance/websites/{id}/pages/return_policy
{"entry_id": "<id of the content entry>"}

Page kinds: owner_info, privacy_policy, return_policy, shipping_policy, payment_policy, inspection_policy, complaint_policy, terms_of_use, general_conditions, and for a marketplace platform_rules, seller_agreement. A linked page counts only once it is published.

4. Checklist

GET /api/v1/compliance/websites/{id}/checklist (MCP compliance.website_checklist) lists every item of the rule moit.checklist.sales or moit.checklist.platform in force, each with a status:

StatusMeaning
metDone.
missingTo do; hint says which endpoint to call.
unknownAutoxide cannot tell yet (e.g. no installed module answers “each product page shows its seller”) — check it yourself.
manualAlways checked by a person (e.g. your account on online.gov.vn).

Each item carries its legal_basis. When the rules change, platform staff import a new version (POST /api/v1/compliance/rules) and the checklist follows — no upgrade needed.

Selling before the procedure is done is shown as “required by law” (selling_before_done: "label"), not blocked: the sources found treat it as an administrative offence with fines, not as a ban on transactions. Check this with a legal professional (decision D-358).

5. Dossier

GET /api/v1/compliance/websites/{id}/dossier (MCP compliance.website_dossier) returns, filled from your data:

  • form: the declaration fields (owner, website, identity verification, linked pages with full URLs, complaints contact);
  • documents: Markdown drafts in Vietnamese — the declaration; for a marketplace also a draft service project plan and draft operating regulations;
  • checklist and a disclaimer.

Complete and review the drafts with a legal professional, then submit on online.gov.vn.

6. Status and logo

Record each step by hand:

POST /api/v1/compliance/websites/{id}/status
{"status": "submitted", "reference": "HS-001"}

Statuses: not_submitted, submitted, needs_supplement, notified (sales website), registered (marketplace), rejected, revoked. For notified / registered, paste the logo code the ministry gave in snippet (or send link_url and image_url). Only a link to online.gov.vn around an image from online.gov.vn is accepted — scripts, event handlers, styles or other domains give compliance.logo_snippet_invalid (422). The code is never stored or returned as sent: Autoxide keeps the two addresses and rebuilds a clean snippet.

7. For the interface (public, no sign-in)

EndpointReturns
GET /api/v1/compliance/public/badgeThe logo of the website being viewed: color (blue / red), link_url, image_url, alt, ready snippet. 404 while the website has no logo.
GET /api/v1/compliance/public/ownerThe owner’s information for the footer. 404 without a legal profile.

The website is found by ?domain=, then the request’s domain, then the website of the unit being viewed (?site=<unit id> or a domain bound to a unit). Both send an ETag; send it back in If-None-Match to get 304.

8. autoxide doctor

The check compliance.moit_readiness warns (never fails) when no website has a legal profile yet, or when a website not yet submitted still lacks checklist items — with the checklist address of each.

Mandatory page templates (content)

The content module ships starting texts for the mandatory pages, in English and Vietnamese:

GET  /api/v1/content/templates
POST /api/v1/content/templates/{key}/drafts
{"locale": "vi", "variables": {"policy": {"return_days": 7}}}
  • Keys: owner_info, privacy_policy, return_policy, shipping_policy, payment_policy, inspection_policy, complaint_policy, terms_of_use, general_conditions; for a marketplace platform_rules, seller_agreement, seller_info.
  • The draft is filled with the legal profile of the unit (owner.*, contact.*, read from compliance), then with the variables you send (they win). Every value goes into text only — it can never add markup. A required variable still missing gives content.template_variables_missing (422) listing them all.
  • The result is a draft page (content.write); review it, publish it, then link it to the website.
  • Every template starts with “Sample content — have a legal professional review it before use”.

Personal data

Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (in force from 1 January 2026 — check the originals with a legal professional). Each tenant is the data controller of its own data.

Purposes and legal bases

GET /purposes (compliance.privacy.read) lists the purposes of processing: the defaults of the legal rule privacy.default_purposes (service, marketing, analytics, legal) merged with the tenant’s own. POST /purposes adds one, PATCH /purposes/{key} changes one (changing a default stores the tenant’s version) — both need compliance.privacy.manage. Each purpose has a category, a legal_basis (consent, contract, legal_obligation, vital_interest, public_interest), the kinds of data, the recipients (name, country) and a description by language. GET /public/purposes (no sign-in) lists the purposes in use for the privacy notice; active tracking providers appear there as recipients, with the country that receives the data.

Retention

Every module declares the personal data it keeps and for how long (Module::retention, read from its settings). GET /retention lists them, each compared with the legal rule privacy.retention_limits: ok, exceeds_limit or forever_not_allowed (only accounting — proof the law requires — may be kept forever). The limits shipped are cautious defaults, not numbers from the law. autoxide doctor warns (compliance.retention) when an item is not ok; lower the setting named in setting.

Processing register

GET /processing-register returns purposes, legal bases, kinds of data, recipients, retention and where the data is kept — the input of a data protection impact assessment, whose official form needs a legal professional.

Not covered yet (before 1.0)

Findings of the first legal review (AI-performed, not legal advice; docs/legal/review-2026-11-r1/) that core does not handle yet. Until they are done, the deployer covers them:

  • Seller identity before selling. The legal rule kyc.required_activities asks for verified sellers, livestreamers and affiliates (E-commerce Law 122/2025/QH15; Decree 248/2026/ND-CP Article 18, electronic identity of sellers from 1 January 2027 — check the originals). When the tenant is a marketplace (kernel.business_model = marketplace), catalog asks KycGate before a product of a seller (any branch other than the tenant’s root) goes active. The seller is the owning branch or a branch above it, below the root (marketplace → seller → store): one verified branch in that chain is enough. An unverified seller gets compliance.kyc_required (403); goods of the root and tenants that sell their own goods are not asked. If module compliance is turned off (for example AUTOXIDE_MODULES=official,-compliance), identity cannot be checked, so goods of a seller cannot go on sale on a marketplace (catalog.seller_verification_unavailable, 403). Only the change to active is checked: goods already on sale stay on sale when the tenant becomes a marketplace, the rule starts to apply or a seller’s verification expires or is revoked — stop them yourself (POST /api/v1/catalog/products/{id}/status). A marketplace that gives each seller its own child tenant instead of a branch is not checked this way: each child tenant sells its own goods. Still open: livestream and affiliate flows (no module yet), and the profile does not record every item Article 18 lists — individuals: full name, date of birth, personal identification number (one source adds residential address); organisations and household businesses: name, head office, identification number, legal representative; foreign individuals: passport (or equivalent) number and issuing country, valid at least 6 more months at review. The profile keeps the verified name and a hash and last 4 digits of the document; the other items stay with the eKYC provider. Check the originals before relying on this.
  • Personal data of children. Law 91/2025/QH15 sets special rules for children and persons under guardianship (consent of a parent or guardian; from age 7 also the child’s — the article and ages need checking). Core has no birth date, age check or guardian consent: a service aimed at children must collect and keep the guardian’s consent itself. Planned after 1.0: a per-tenant setting, self-declared age at sign-up and consent given on someone’s behalf in the consent ledger.
  • Data sent abroad by providers. The AI providers of module ai, mail, SMS and file storage may process personal data outside Vietnam; they are not added to the processing register by themselves. Add each provider as a recipient (name, country) of the purposes that use it (POST /purposes, PATCH /purposes/{key}), and prepare the impact assessment for transfers abroad (Decree 356/2025/ND-CP — form and exemptions for small businesses need checking). Planned after 1.0: modules declare their processors (Module::processors) and autoxide doctor warns about recipients abroad.

Data residency

A tenant can be required to keep its data in one country (Cybersecurity Law 116/2025/QH15, in force 1 July 2026, replacing the Cybersecurity Law 2018 and the Network Information Security Law 2015 — secondary sources, to check; its implementing decree, reported as Decree 333/2026/ND-CP, replaces Decree 53/2022/ND-CP — not confirmed. Who must keep data in Vietnam needs a legal check):

autoxide placement country main VN          # record where a placement's servers are

PUT /api/v1/compliance/residency
{"tenant_id": "…", "data_residency": "VN"}

Only platform staff with compliance.privacy.manage set it. It is refused (tenant_residency_violation, 409) while the tenant’s placement is not in that country or its country is unknown; moving the tenant later to a placement elsewhere is refused with the same code, and so is changing the placement’s country. GET /residency shows the tenant’s state; autoxide doctor warns (compliance.data_residency) about any tenant not met.

Personal data breaches: the 72-hour runbook

The report to the authority is due 72 hours after detection (legal rule privacy.incident, data with an effective date). Do this as soon as a breach is suspected:

  1. Record it at once — POST /incidents with title, severity (low…critical) and detected_at (when it was detected, not when it started). The answer gives report_due_at. Needs compliance.incidents.manage.
  2. Set who is reminded — the tenant policy incident.alert_emails (PUT /policies/incident.alert_emails with a list of addresses). A job every 15 minutes sends a mail at 24, 48, 60 and 68 hours and at the deadline (each once), and emits compliance.incident_deadline_near.
  3. Contain and log — PATCH /incidents/{id} with status: "contained", the kinds of data and the number of people; add each finding and action with POST /incidents/{id}/entries (the log only grows).
  4. Draft the notifications — GET /incidents/{id}/draft?audience=authority&locale=vi (and audience=subjects for the people affected). These are drafts filled from the incident and the privacy.contact policy: complete the missing parts, have them reviewed, send them yourself.
  5. Record the report — PATCH /incidents/{id} with authority_notified_at and authority_reference (the breach becomes reported), then subjects_notified_at when people were told; closed at the end.

GET /incidents?overdue=true lists breaches past their deadline, and autoxide doctor (compliance.privacy_deadlines) warns about them. The deployment guide points here (DEPLOY.md).

Requests of data subjects received through other channels

A request a user makes in their account (export, delete) runs at once. A request received by email, post, phone or from an authority is recorded so it is not lost:

  • POST /subject-requests with kind (access, erasure, rectification, objection, restriction, withdrawal), channel (email, post, phone, authority, other), subject (a user id, an email address or a phone number) and received_at. due_at comes from the legal rule privacy.subject_requests in force when it was received: 72 hours, stricter than the 2 working days to answer of Decree 356/2025/ND-CP Article 5 (which replaced Decree 13/2023/ND-CP). Version 2 of the rule also lists the days to carry the request out by kind (fulfil_within_days, from secondary sources) for reference; Autoxide does not track that second deadline yet — check the original texts.
  • POST /subject-requests/{id}/fulfil: access of a user returns the personal data of every module (send it to the person; it is not stored); erasure / withdrawal of an email or phone number empties it from the consent ledger (the hashed key and the records stay as proof, so nothing is sent again). Erasing a whole account is DELETE /api/v1/auth/users/{id}.
  • PATCH /subject-requests/{id} sets in_progress or rejected (with a reason). GET /subject-requests?overdue=true lists late ones. All need compliance.privacy.manage.

When an account is deleted (auth.user_deleted), compliance deletes its verified phone number and pending codes and removes the user from cookie consent records; proof of consents, accepted documents and the identity verification result stay for the time the law requires. The personal data export of a user (GET /api/v1/auth/me/export) includes the compliance part.

Reports of infringements and consumer complaints

Intellectual property infringements, counterfeits and prohibited goods are reported as cases; so are consumer complaints. A case goes: intake → (hide the target) → answer of the reported party → decision, with deadlines and a log that only grows. Basis — check the original texts: E-commerce Law 122/2025/QH15 and Decree 248/2026/ND-CP (from 1 July 2026; take-down on request of an authority, Decree 85/2021/ND-CP before), Intellectual Property Law, Consumer Protection Law 19/2023/QH15 and Decree 55/2024/ND-CP (complaints).

WhoEndpoints (under /api/v1/compliance)
Anyone (public form, tenant from the domain)POST /public/reports, POST /public/complaints, GET /public/complaints, GET /public/cases/{id}?token=, GET /public/moderation-reasons
Staff (compliance.cases.read / .handle)GET|POST /cases, GET /cases/{id}, POST /cases/{id}/entries, …/restrict, …/restore, …/decision, …/close
The reported party (signed in)GET /respondent/cases, POST /cases/{id}/responses
Staff (compliance.disclosures.manage)GET|POST /disclosures

Intake. The public forms ask a form token first (GET /api/v1/antispam/token?scope=compliance:reports, or compliance:complaints) and are limited per network block and email per hour (AUTOXIDE_COMPLIANCE_PUBLIC_REPORTS_PER_IP_PER_HOUR, default 5). The target is the reported object as <module>.<type>:<id> (e.g. content.entry:…, comments.comment:…; a product once step 41 exists); it must exist. The sender gets tracking_token once — only its SHA-256 is kept — and a receipt mail when they gave an address (link: AUTOXIDE_COMPLIANCE_CASE_TRACKING_URL with {case} and {token}). Staff record cases received by letter (POST /cases, reporter_kind rights_holder or authority).

Deadlines come from the legal rule cases.deadlines: hours to acknowledge, working days to answer and to decide by kind (consumer complaints: 3 and 7), and 24 hours to decide a request of an authority. Working days skip Saturdays, Sundays and the holidays listed in the rule (Vietnam time); lunar new year and make-up days are entered each year. The hourly schedule compliance.case_deadlines sends the event compliance.case_overdue and a mail to the complaints.contact policy’s email, once per deadline.

Hiding the target. A request of an authority hides the target at once; so does an intellectual property or counterfeit report when the tenant sets the policy cases.auto_restrict to true. Staff hide or show it by hand (/restrict, /restore). compliance never touches the tables of other modules: it sends compliance.target_restricted / compliance.target_restored (kernel contract autoxide_kernel::moderation), and the owning module hides the object — content answers 451 content.restricted and leaves the page out of lists, sitemap, feed and search; comments hides the comment and restores its former status. The target is shown again only when no other case still hides it.

The reported party is the owner of the target (from the kernel’s resource directory, e.g. the author of the page) and anyone with compliance.cases.respond on the reported unit (respondent_org_id, a seller on a marketplace). They see their cases without the reporter’s contact details and answer before respond_by; later gives compliance.response_closed (409).

Decision. A signed-in person with compliance.cases.handle decides (upheld or partially_upheld keep the target hidden; rejected shows it again); API keys get compliance.session_required. The reason is shown to both sides and mailed to the reporter. There is no MCP tool for deciding.

Disclosures to authorities. POST /disclosures (signed-in person, compliance.disclosures.manage) records the authority, its request number, the legal basis and the names of the fields given, and returns the values once — from the seller’s legal profile and identity verification kept by compliance. The record and the event compliance.disclosure_recorded never hold the values.

MCP tools: compliance.list_cases, compliance.get_case (read), compliance.add_case_note, compliance.restrict_case_target.

Consumer protection

  • Hiding reviews. Content of consumers may be hidden only for the reasons of the legal rule moderation.hide_reasons (spam, illegal_content, personal_data, unrelated, offensive — to check). Modules that moderate reviews ask the trait ModerationPolicy (autoxide_kernel::moderation) before hiding — orders maps its own reasons (unlawful_content → illegal_content, abusive_language → offensive, spam_or_advertising → spam, not_about_product → unrelated), so a reason a tenant adds to its list cannot hide a review while compliance is installed (compliance.hide_reason_not_allowed, 422) and record the reason in their own event; the list is public at GET /public/moderation-reasons. A negative but lawful review stays visible.
  • General conditions of trade are a versioned legal document of kind general_conditions, public at GET /public/documents/general_conditions.
  • Complaint channel. GET /public/complaints gives the website’s complaint page the contact (policy complaints.contact) and the deadlines; complaints are cases of kind consumer_complaint.

AI labels

Content written or edited from AI suggestions carries ai_assisted (content) and image alt texts from suggestions carry alt_ai_assisted (media), returned by the public endpoints so the interface labels them (Law on Artificial Intelligence 134/2025/QH15, from 1 March 2026 — check the decree). See CONTENT.md and MEDIA.md.

Tracking tags and cookie consent

A tenant adds the measurement tags of advertising platforms without pasting scripts, and they run only after the visitor consents. (Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15, Decree 356/2025/ND-CP, Consumer Protection Law 19/2023/QH15 — whether analytics cookies need consent, and the impact assessment for transfers abroad, need a legal check; the design takes the strict reading: ask first.)

GET /tracking-providers lists the built-in providers; the tenant only enters the ID:

A wrong ID gives compliance.tracking_id_invalid with the expected form; an unknown provider compliance.tracking_provider_unknown.

Banner

PUT /cookie-settings/{org_id} sets enabled, the optional categories asked about (preferences, analytics, marketing; necessary is always on and carries no tag) and position. Categories of active tags are always asked. Changing the categories or the active providers raises policy_version: visitors are asked again. GET /cookie-consents/stats?org_id= gives totals of the last 30 days, never single records.

For the interface

GET  /api/v1/compliance/public/consent-config?granted=analytics&locale=vi
POST /api/v1/compliance/public/cookie-consents
{"consent_id": "<random, kept in a necessary cookie>", "categories": ["analytics"], "policy_version": 3}

The contract with the interface:

  • Nothing loads before consent. Without granted the config returns no tag; with it, only the active tags of those categories. Read the visitor’s choice from your necessary cookie and pass it.
  • Reject is as easy as accept. Show texts.reject_all as prominently as texts.accept_all, on the first layer; no category is preselected (preselected: false). texts.legal is not overridable.
  • Record the choice with POST /public/cookie-consents (no sign-in; limited per network block by AUTOXIDE_COMPLIANCE_COOKIE_CONSENTS_PER_IP_PER_HOUR). It stores the random id, the categories, the version and a hash of the network block — never the raw IP — for the days of privacy.consent_proof_days. A signed-in visitor’s choice also goes to the consent ledger (purposes analytics / marketing, scope website:<unit id>). An old policy_version gives compliance.cookie_policy_changed (409): show the banner again.
  • Do not slow the page. Load tags async, after the page has rendered (loading: after_consent_deferred); add csp_hosts to the Content-Security-Policy; never load tags on sign-in or account pages; on checkout pages send only the standard conversion events (view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase).
  • Sending conversions from the server to ad platforms is not built (it needs its own legal check on personal data transfers).
