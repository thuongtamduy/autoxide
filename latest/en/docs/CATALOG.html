Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Catalog

Tiếng Việt

The catalog module holds the products every commerce module reads: products with variants, a category tree, and price lists with a full price history. Inventory, promotions, shipping, orders and sales channels read products and prices through autoxide_catalog::api::Catalog, never through the catalog_… tables. Everything is under /api/v1/catalog; every endpoint is described in docs/openapi.json, and the same operations are MCP tools (catalog.*).

Products and variants

  • POST /products creates a product as a draft together with its first variant. kind is physical (shipped, stocked), digital, service or bundle. tax_category is a code of the tax engine (GET /api/v1/tax/categories), e.g. vat.standard.
  • Texts are per language (PUT /products/{id}/translations/{locale}); the languages allowed are AUTOXIDE_CATALOG_LOCALES. Each translation has its public path, /<AUTOXIDE_CATALOG_PRODUCT_PATH_PREFIX>/<slug>. An empty slug is made from the name, with -2, -3… when taken. The description is plain text (blank lines separate paragraphs); HTML is not interpreted.
  • Options (colour, size…) are set with PUT /products/{id}/options: up to 3 options of up to 100 values. Adding an option gives every existing variant the option’s first value. Each variant holds one value of each option, and no two variants share a combination (catalog.option_combination_taken).
  • SKUs and barcodes are unique in a tenant (catalog.sku_taken, catalog.barcode_taken); SKUs are compared ignoring case and spaces. A numeric barcode of 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits must have a right GTIN check digit.
  • Sale mode per variant: in_stock, backorder or preorder (with preorder_available_on after today and an optional preorder_limit).
  • Images are public media of the media module (PUT /products/{id}/media).
  • Status (POST /products/{id}/status, permission catalog.products.publish): draft → active or archived; active ↔ inactive; any → archived; archived → draft. Going on sale needs a tax category, a name and an active variant. A product that was on sale once cannot be deleted, only archived.
  • Bundles (kind: bundle) list their components with PUT /variants/{id}/components; a bundle cannot contain a bundle, itself or a service.

The number of products is the plan limit catalog.products (archived products count). Plan features: catalog.variants (more than one variant), catalog.price_lists (more than one price list), catalog.bundles, catalog.digital, catalog.ai.

Categories

One tree per tenant, at most 7 levels deep (/categories, permission catalog.categories.manage). POST /categories/{id}/move moves a category with everything below it; moving it under itself is refused (catalog.category_cycle). Filtering products by a category includes its subcategories.

Attributes, restricted goods and readiness

  • Attributes (/attributes, permission catalog.categories.manage) are typed: text, integer, decimal (with scale), boolean, date, option, options. Products set their values with PUT /products/{id}/attributes.
  • A category lists its attributes with PUT /categories/{id}/attributes; an attribute can be required, from effective_from until effective_to, for the category and (by default) its subcategories. Required attributes are data: new labelling rules are added with their start date.
  • Prohibited and conditional goods are shared rules with their legal basis (GET /restriction-rules). The operator loads them with autoxide task catalog.load_restrictions (the vn pack ships with the server; the same command creates sample product-label attributes such as origin and use-by date for a tenant). Link rules to a category with PUT /categories/{id}/restrictions. The rules were compiled from secondary sources — check them against the source texts with a legal professional.
  • A conditional product needs valid documents (/products/{id}/documents, permission catalog.documents.manage), e.g. an alcohol retail licence.
  • Every product has a readiness: restricted (prohibited goods), missing_attributes, missing_documents or ready. Only ready products go on sale. Filter with ?readiness= and count with GET /products/counts?group_by=readiness. Expired documents are noticed by a daily job.
  • Going on sale also checks that the tax category exists in the tax engine for the unit’s tax profile (catalog.tax_category_unknown).

Prices

A price list belongs to an organization unit (a chain or a store) and applies there and below. It has a currency, tax_inclusive, an audience (consumer or business), an optional customer group and a priority.

Price rows are only ever added. A new price is a new row with its own valid_from; a future valid_from schedules a price, and valid_to makes it temporary — when it ends, the earlier price applies again. The history is GET /price-lists/{id}/prices. POST /price-lists/{id}/prices adds up to 500 rows under your reference: sending the same reference and content again returns the rows already written.

Which list wins. For a store, customer group and time, the lists that apply are the active lists in the currency, at the store or a unit above it, whose customer group (and company, channel, market) is empty or matches. They are ordered: the unit closest to the store first, then a matching company, customer group, channel and market, then higher priority, then id. Each variant takes its price from the first list in that order that has one. GET /variants/{id}/price?org_id=…&currency=… shows the price and the ordered candidate lists.

Consumer prices in Viet Nam must be in VND and include tax when the unit has a Vietnamese tax profile (Price Law 16/2023/QH15 — check the source text with a legal professional before relying on it).

Derived price lists take their prices from a source list (derived_from; one level only, catalog.derived_chain), adjusted by adjust_bp (basis points, signed: -1000 = 10% off) and adjust_minor, then rounded by a rounding rule (/rounding-rules: multiples of step_minor, or k × step_minor + ending_minor, e.g. step 10,000 and ending 9,000 give 129,000 and 199,000). A job writes real price rows into the derived list whenever the source gets prices, so reading a price never computes a formula. A manual row in the derived list pins that variant’s price while it is in effect.

Bulk price changes (/price-lists/{id}/changes): create (set, percent or amount, for categories, products or variants, optionally temporary with valid_to) → preview (counts, 20 sample rows, price-floor violations; nothing is written) → approve (catalog.prices.approve; with the unit setting catalog.price_change_requires_second_person on, someone other than the maker must approve) → a job writes the rows (source = bulk). Undo a change with a new change back to the old prices.

Price floors (/price-guards): a minimum price and / or a minimum margin over unit cost, per unit and optionally per category, acting as warn (the price is saved with a warning) or block (catalog.price_below_floor). The rule of the closest unit wins; within a unit, the rule of the closest category wins. Unit costs come from the inventory.cost_changed event.

Public reads for websites

No sign-in needed (/api/v1/catalog/public/…). Only products that are active, have a price on the website asking and a text in the language asked; the website comes from the domain or ?site=<unit>. Every answer has an ETag (send it back in If-None-Match to get 304) and Cache-Control: public, no-cache.

Product cards carry variant_summary (“3 sizes”), brand (the attribute with code brand) and unit_price (price per kg, l, m or piece) when a variant has a net content (net_quantity + net_unit, e.g. 500 and g). Product pages give each variant a path with ?variant=<id>, unit_price, and for a temporary price price_valid_until and regular_price (the price just before it) — what structured data (Product, ProductGroup, Offer) needs; the GTIN is barcode.

Search helpers:

  • GET /public/search/suggest?q=…&locale=vi — suggestions as the shopper types (products, categories, and what other modules add), exact accents first, at most 10.
  • GET /public/products/facets — takes the filters of /public/products and counts products per attribute value and per seller (the counts of one filter ignore that filter).
  • GET / PUT /synonyms — the tenant’s synonym groups ([["áo thun", "áo phông", "tee"]]); search and suggestions use them (catalog.categories.manage).

Best sellers and ratings come from module orders: each completed order (orders.order_completed) counts its products once (a redelivered event is not counted twice), over a rolling 90-day window that a daily job (catalog.refresh_sales) moves forward; review events carry the product’s current review count and star total. The same orders feed “frequently bought together”. “Similar” needs the ai module with embeddings on (AUTOXIDE_EMBEDDING_PROVIDER and the other AUTOXIDE_EMBEDDING_* settings): each product on sale gets one vector per language, and its list of similar products is computed when it is indexed, not on each read.

Which products a website shows: those of its own unit, of the units above it, and of sellers below it (seller.own = false on the card). A store hides a product of its chain with PUT /stores/{org_id}/overrides/{product_id} {"hidden": true} (catalog.products.write at the store).

Prices on these pages are the current prices of the website’s price list (the closest unit with an active price list), without customer group. They are kept ready in a derived table rebuilt by a background job after every change of price, status, price list or store choice, and every minute for scheduled prices that just started or ended (event catalog.price_effective). If the public lists ever look out of date, run autoxide task catalog.rebuild_storefront [tenant=<slug>].

Collections

A collection groups products for a page of the website (“Summer sale”, “New this month”). Manage them under /collections (catalog.collections.manage at the collection’s unit; reading needs catalog.products.read); PATCH and PUT /collections/{id}/items need If-Match.

  • kind: manual — chosen products in order: PUT /collections/{id}/items {"product_ids": [...]} (at most 500) replaces the list.
  • kind: rule — products matching every condition of rules (at most 10): {"category_ids": [...], "attributes": {"color": ["red"]}, "price_min": 100000, "price_max": 500000, "new_within_days": 30, "on_promotion": true, "match": "all"}. Prices are in the smallest unit of the website’s price list currency; on_promotion needs module promotions (catalog.rule_needs_module).
  • sort: manual (chosen order, manual collections only), newest, price_asc, price_desc, best_selling; equal values keep a stable order by id on both databases.
  • texts by language: title, slug (unique in the language), description, meta_title, meta_description; media_id (a public picture); status (draft, active, archived) and an optional starts_at / ends_at.
  • ai_assisted: the texts were written with an AI’s suggestion; the public page carries it so the website shows an AI label (Vietnam’s Law on Artificial Intelligence — check the original text). MCP tools set it by default; a person clears it with PATCH after rewriting the texts.

The website reads GET /api/v1/catalog/public/collections/{slug}?locale=vi (public; site or the domain): the collection’s texts and one page of its products, with the same cards as /public/products, only products still shown on that website, in the collection’s order. Only active collections in their time window, of the website or a unit above it, are public. Banners and menus point to a collection as catalog.collection of the object directory (/collections/<slug>). MCP tools: catalog.list_collections, catalog.save_collection (drafts only: a person sets a collection active). A worked home-page example: docs/guides/storefront.md.

For other modules

#![allow(unused)]
fn main() {
let catalog = context.require::<dyn autoxide_catalog::api::Catalog>()?;
}

variants, variant_by_code, prices (price in effect at a time), reference_prices (the price in effect just before a time — for “price before the discount”), price_floors and bundle_components. Every call takes at most 500 ids and runs a fixed number of queries. Events are listed in autoxide_catalog::api::events.

A module registered after catalog plugs in through dyn CatalogExtensions: a promotions module gives the public lists their automatic sale prices with set_sale_price_source (a SalePriceSource), and emits promotions.automatic_prices_changed (after.variant_ids) when they change. A marketplace module can refuse to put a product on sale with set_listing_gate (a ListingGate); the status change then answers 422 catalog.listing_denied.

catalog.display_price_changed tells when the public price of a product on a website changes (the automatic sale price when there is one, otherwise the lowest variant price; null when it is not on sale there; never the crossed-out compare_at). A rebuild that leaves the price as it was sends nothing.

Import and export (CSV)

Both run in the background and need file storage (module files); without it they answer 503 catalog.storage_unavailable. Files are kept 7 days.

  • POST /imports?org_id=<unit> with the file itself as the body (Content-Type: text/csv, UTF-8, at most 20 MiB and 50 000 rows; catalog.import). Columns, first line, any order: sku (required), name, locale, short_description, description, kind, tax_category, barcode, weight_grams. A known SKU updates that variant and the product’s text in locale (the address stays); a new SKU creates a draft product with one variant (needs name and tax_category). Nothing is put on sale by an import, so importing the same file again changes nothing. Each row is checked like a manual change with your rights: a bad row is reported and the others are written.
  • GET /imports/{id}: status is queued, running, succeeded, succeeded_with_errors (see errors: line, column, code, message), rejected (the file is not valid; nothing was written) or failed. Only the person who started it sees it. Products with several variants or options are completed through the API after the import.
  • POST /exports {"org_id": …} (catalog.export): every variant of the products you can read, with the same columns, so the file can be edited and imported back. Cells a spreadsheet would run as a formula (=, +, -, @) start with '; an import removes that ' again. When GET /exports/{id} says succeeded, download content_path.

MCP tools: catalog.start_import, catalog.import_status.

AI drafts of descriptions and SEO

POST /products/{id}/suggestions {"locale": "vi"} (catalog.products.write, plan feature catalog.ai, the ai module on) asks the model for a short description, a description and SEO texts using only what the product’s texts already say. The result is a draft: nothing on the product changes. POST /suggestions/{id}/apply writes it into that language’s text (name and address unchanged) and marks the product ai_assisted; POST /suggestions/{id}/discard drops it. MCP tool: catalog.suggest_copy.

Draft a product from photos

POST /products/from-images {"media_ids": […], "locale": "vi", "tax_category": "vat.standard", "category_id": null, "org_id": null} (catalog.products.write, plan feature catalog.ai, an AI model that reads images) sends 1 to 8 photos of the media library — server-made thumbnails without metadata, never the originals — to the model, in the background. Follow Location (GET /products/from-images/{id}) until status is succeeded: product_id is a new draft product marked ai_assisted, with name, descriptions and SEO texts, a category of your tree (or the one you gave; a category outside your tree is dropped), the attribute values of that category the model was sure of, and the public photos as its images. Prices, stock and barcode are left for you; the SKU is a temporary AI-… code. Nothing is put on sale. (Refusing photos generated by AI as product images comes with step 67, which marks them.)

Product Q&A, menus and other modules

Products and categories are in the kernel’s object directory, so other modules can point at them without reading catalog tables:

  • catalog.product: public when on sale (active) with a text in the language asked; title, short description, address /<prefix>/<slug> and first image. Product questions and answers use module comments with this target type. Suggested settings: AUTOXIDE_COMMENTS_TARGET_TYPES including catalog.product, AUTOXIDE_COMMENTS_VERIFIED_ONLY_TYPES=catalog.product (verified accounts only, Decree 147/2024 — check the scope with the original text) and AUTOXIDE_COMMENTS_MODERATION_BY_TYPE=catalog.product:user=pre (questions wait for review). Archiving or deleting a product closes and hides its questions. An author who can manage the product (catalog.products.write on its unit) gets the badge seller; module orders adds verified_buyer.
  • catalog.category: for website menus (module menus) — names and addresses (/categories/<slug>) by language, children in display order, only visible categories. A “best sellers” block in a menu is the public product list filtered by category and sorted best_selling.

Image texts by language: PUT /products/{id}/media/{media_id}/alts/{locale} {"alt": "…"} (with If-Match; DELETE removes it). Product pages in that language show it; otherwise the product’s own alt in its default language, then the media library’s text in that language. Removing an image from the product removes its texts.

What differs between variants

PUT /variants/{id}/attributes sets attribute values on one variant — how many people it serves, what is in the box, care instructions, dishwasher safe — using the tenant’s attributes (POST /attributes), so nothing is fixed to one trade. Text values may carry translations: {"attribute_id": …, "value": "Rửa tay", "texts": {"en": "Hand wash"}}. The public product page lists them under each variant (variants[].attributes) in the language asked.

Digital products

A product of kind digital is delivered as download links instead of being shipped (plan feature catalog.digital).

  1. Upload the file with POST /api/v1/files (or a direct upload, /api/v1/files/uploads).
  2. Attach it to the variant: POST /variants/{id}/digital-assets {"file_id": …, "download_limit": 5, "grant_days": 30} (catalog.products.write). The content is copied, so deleting the uploaded file later does not affect buyers. A digital product cannot go on sale without a file (catalog.digital_asset_missing).
  3. When module orders confirms a paid order (orders.order_confirmed with payment_status paid), each order line of a digital variant gets one download right per file: download_limit downloads, valid grant_days days. Receiving the event again gives nothing more. Cancelling the order, or a refund not tied to a return, revokes the rights at once.
  4. Buyers with an account list their rights with GET /downloads and spend one with POST /downloads/{id}. Staff with catalog.downloads.manage see an order’s rights (GET /orders/{id}/downloads), revoke them (POST /orders/{id}/downloads/revoke) or give new tokens to send to a buyer without an account (POST /orders/{id}/downloads/reissue, the only time a token_path is shown).
  5. Spending a download (POST /public/downloads/{token}, no sign-in) answers a signed file address valid AUTOXIDE_CATALOG_DOWNLOAD_LINK_SECONDS; it accepts Range to resume and does not spend more downloads. Addresses are signed with a key of the tenant: one tenant’s address does not work on another’s domain.

Services (service) are neither shipped nor stocked; bundles (bundle) take stock from each component (PUT /variants/{id}/components); pre-orders use the variant’s sale_mode.
