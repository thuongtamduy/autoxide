Customer support (module support)

The support module takes support requests from customers — questions about an order, a product, an account or a payment — and lets the shop’s staff answer them until they are done. Each request is a ticket with messages back and forth. Tickets belong to an organization unit (a shop, a branch, a seller’s store on a marketplace), so chains and marketplaces split the work by branch like every other record.

Vietnamese: SUPPORT.vi.md.

What it does not do: consumer complaints with legal deadlines live in module compliance (a ticket is handed over there, never copied), disputes between buyers and sellers in marketplace, returns in orders. Live chat is a later module.

Permissions

PermissionAllows
support.tickets.readSeeing the tickets of the units in your scope, with messages and internal notes
support.tickets.replyAnswering, internal notes, opening tickets for customers, status, priority, assignee, links
support.tickets.manageHiding messages, handing tickets over as consumer complaints or to the platform
support.settings.manageSupport policy, teams, routing rules and saved replies of the units in your scope
support.auto_reply.manageApproving saved replies for automatic answers

Every permission works per organization unit: staff of a branch see the tickets of that branch and the branches below it; a marketplace seller with a grant at their store sees only that store’s tickets. With scope own, staff see the tickets assigned to them.

Customers need no permission: any signed-in account sees its own tickets at /api/v1/support/me/tickets.

Tickets

  • From the website — POST /api/v1/support/public/tickets. Signed in: the ticket belongs to the account. Guest: give email or phone and the anti-spam fields of the form (GET /api/v1/antispam/token?scope=support:tickets). The answer carries access_token once — the guest keeps it to see (GET /public/tickets/{id}?token=) and answer (POST /public/tickets/{id}/messages) this one ticket. A 6-digit code goes to the email or phone; the guest confirms it (POST /public/tickets/{id}/verify) before writing a second message.
  • By staff — POST /api/v1/support/tickets with the customer’s account, email or phone (a phone call, a visit).
  • From another module — through the trait SupportIntake (e.g. the order page); the same reference never makes two tickets.
  • After sign-up — POST /api/v1/support/me/claim attaches the verified guest tickets with the account’s verified phone number, or with the email / phone of a verified guest ticket whose token the customer sends.

Statuses: open → pending_agent (the customer wrote) ⇄ pending_customer (staff answered) → solved → closed. A customer writing on a solved ticket opens it again; closed is final (open a new ticket with follow_up_of). Only allowed steps are taken (support.invalid_transition).

Messages are never edited. A mistaken message is hidden with a reason (POST /tickets/{id}/messages/{message_id}/hide, support.tickets.manage); the customer sees that a message was hidden. Internal notes (internal: true) are for staff only and notify the colleagues listed in mentions. Signed-in customers attach media items they uploaded themselves (at most 5 per message).

Settings per unit

All settings are data of an organization unit and apply to the units below it unless they have their own.

  • Policy — PUT /api/v1/support/policies/{org_id}: ticket categories, minutes to the first answer and to solve per priority (low, normal, high, urgent, counted in business time), business hours and days off, UTC offset, days before a solved ticket closes, whether guests may open tickets, email replies, automatic answers. Defaults: Monday–Friday 08:00–17:00 (UTC+7), first answer within 8 business hours and solved within 3 business days for normal.
  • Teams — POST /api/v1/support/teams, members with PUT /teams/{id}/members/{user_id} (accepting new tickets; members switch it themselves). A member must be able to answer tickets of the unit.
  • Routing rules — POST /api/v1/support/routing-rules (category, channel → team). A new ticket goes to the team of the first matching rule (nearest unit first) and round-robin to the member waiting longest who still has support.tickets.reply at the ticket’s unit.
  • Saved replies — POST /api/v1/support/macros with text per language and the variables {customer_name}, {ticket_number}, {subject}; fill one in for a ticket with POST /tickets/{id}/macros/{macro_id}/render.

Deadlines and work

The first-answer and solve deadlines are computed when a ticket opens and when its priority changes. While the ticket waits for the customer, the clock stops; when the customer writes, the solve deadline moves by the business time waited. The job support.sla_check (every minute) marks late tickets once (breached, event support.ticket_breached); support.auto_close closes solved tickets after the policy’s days. Tickets waiting for staff show in the work inbox (GET /api/v1/kernel/work, source support.tickets). GET /api/v1/support/reports/summary?from=&to= counts tickets, solved and late ones, median times and the average rating.

Links, hand-over, ratings

  • POST /tickets/{id}/links {"target": "orders.order:<id>"} links a record of any module (it must exist); the order page lists related tickets with GET /tickets?link=orders.order:<id>.
  • POST /tickets/{id}/escalate {"to": "consumer_complaint"} hands the ticket to compliance as a consumer complaint with legal deadlines: the ticket keeps case_id and closes as escalated; calling again never opens a second case. Customers who want to complain send it directly to POST /api/v1/compliance/public/complaints. {"to": "platform"} hands a seller’s ticket to the marketplace’s staff.
  • Once solved, customers rate the support from 1 to 5 (POST /me/tickets/{id}/satisfaction, or the public endpoint with the token).

AI and email

  • POST /tickets/{id}/reply-suggestions drafts an answer with module ai from the ticket’s messages (emails and phone numbers removed) and the unit’s saved replies. It is only a draft: staff edit it and send it with ai_assisted: true, and the customer sees the label.
  • Email replies (off by default; turn on inbound_email in the policy): an inbound mail module gives replies to support+<key>@your-domain to the trait InboundMail; the key of a ticket comes from InboundMail::reply_address_key. Mail from anyone but the customer, to an unknown key or a closed ticket is dropped; mail never opens a new ticket. Quoted earlier mail is cut.

Automatic answers (off by default)

Turn on auto_reply in a unit’s policy. When a customer writes, the AI only classifies the question; rules in the server decide, and only two kinds of text ever reach the customer:

  1. An approved saved reply, word for word with its variables filled in. Approve one with PUT /api/v1/support/macros/{id}/auto-reply {"enabled": true, "questions": {...}, "review_due_on": "YYYY-MM-DD"} (support.auto_reply.manage); after the review day it is no longer used, and editing its text withdraws the approval.
  2. A sentence built from exact data by a module that attached a FactSource (e.g. the status of the customer’s own order); it answers nothing when it is not sure the customer owns the data.

An answer is sent only when the classification is confident enough (auto_reply_min_confidence_bp, default 9000), carries no risk flag, the question has no blocked word (built-in: complaints, refunds, returns, cancellations, payment problems, personal data, legal words, asking for a person — plus the unit’s auto_reply_blocked_terms), and fewer than auto_reply_max_per_thread (default 2) automatic answers came in a row. Otherwise staff answer as usual, with an AI draft prepared. Automatic messages have auto_reply: true: label them “Automatic answer” and show a “Talk to a person” button (POST /me/tickets/{id}/handoff, or the public one with the token), which stops automatic answers on that ticket. Customers rate an answer (…/auto-replies/{reply_id}/feedback); a saved reply reported wrong AUTOXIDE_SUPPORT_AUTO_REPLY_FLAG_LIMIT (3) times in 7 days stops being used. Try a question with POST /api/v1/support/auto-reply/preview (nothing is sent) and read the log at GET /api/v1/support/auto-replies.

Personal data

Deleting an account anonymizes its tickets (name, contact and the customer’s text are removed) and keeps them for statistics. The customer’s tickets and messages are part of the personal data export. Legal basis to check with a qualified person: Law 91/2025/QH15 on personal data protection, Law 19/2023/QH15 on consumer protection, Law 134/2025/QH15 on artificial intelligence (labelling AI-written answers).
