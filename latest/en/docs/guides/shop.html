Autoxide documentation v0.20.0

Opening a shop: your first order from product to e-invoice

This guide takes a fresh quickstart server to a first real sale: a product with a price and stock, a guest buyer’s cart, delivery quoted by Giao Hàng Nhanh (GHN), payment with MoMo, an SMS through eSMS for each step, the parcel booked with GHN, and the e-invoice issued through SePay eInvoice once the parcel is delivered. Every command is a curl call you can paste. The same flow runs as a test on SQLite and PostgreSQL on every build (crates/app/src/e2e/shop.rs).

Vietnamese: shop.vi.md.

Experimental connectors. The four provider adapters (MoMo, GHN, eSMS, SePay eInvoice) were written from each provider’s public documentation and are not yet verified against the live services. Use each provider’s sandbox first, then a small real order. Money always goes straight from the buyer to your own MoMo account; Autoxide never holds it.

What you need

  • A server from the quickstart, reachable from the internet over HTTPS (MoMo and GHN send their notifications to it). For a test on your computer, a tunnel (for example cloudflared or ngrok) to http://127.0.0.1:8080 is enough.
  • An account at each provider, in your shop’s name: a MoMo business account (Partner Code, Access Key, Secret Key), a GHN shop (API token and ShopId), an eSMS account with a registered brandname (ApiKey, SecretKey) and a SePay eInvoice account (client id, client secret, provider account id). Each guide below says where to find these values.
  • curl and jq (the examples read answers with jq).
  • No provider account yet? Go through Try it without real accounts first: the same steps inside a sandbox, where every partner is simulated.
  • Before selling to the public in Vietnam: notify your sales website to the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Decree 52/2013/ND-CP as amended) and publish your terms; see the compliance module. This guide does not replace legal advice.

1. Turn on the connectors

The quickstart runs the official modules only. Add these lines to .env next to compose.quickstart.yml, then start again:

cat >> .env <<'EOF'
AUTOXIDE_MODULES=official,momo,ghn,esms,einvoice_sepay
AUTOXIDE_HTTP_PUBLIC_URL=https://shop-api.example.com
EOF
docker compose -f compose.quickstart.yml up -d

AUTOXIDE_HTTP_PUBLIC_URL is the HTTPS address at which the internet reaches your server (your tunnel’s address when testing). The quickstart already loaded the reference data this guide needs: Vietnamese administrative units (address.import) and the tax pack (tax.import_pack).

Sign in and keep the token (it lasts 15 minutes; sign in again when a call answers 401). Storing a provider account needs a recent sign-in, so sign in again before step 2 if it has been a while.

API=http://127.0.0.1:8080
TOKEN=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/auth/login" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"email":"admin@example.com","password":"<your password>"}' | jq -r .data.access_token)
ORG=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/organizations/units?limit=1" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" | jq -r '.data[0].id')

ORG is your shop: the root unit of your organization tree.

2. Connect your provider accounts

Every value is stored for your shop only; secrets are encrypted and never shown again. The details of each provider are in its own guide: MoMo, GHN, eSMS, SePay eInvoice.

MoMo — payments:

for pair in partner_code=MOMOXXXX access_key=<Access Key> secret_key=<Secret Key>; do
  curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/kernel/settings/momo.connection.account.${pair%%=*}" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d "{\"value\": \"${pair#*=}\"}"
done

The page a buyer returns to after paying must be one of your shop’s own addresses: an https:// address on a domain you verified (POST /api/v1/kernel/domains), or an origin you list here — your website, your app’s universal link domain, http://localhost:3000 while developing. Any other return_url is refused, so a payment link cannot send your buyers to someone else’s site:

curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/kernel/settings/ledger.payments.return_origins" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"value": "https://shop.example.com"}'

eSMS — the SMS sent to buyers. Until you set it, SMS notifications are skipped with the reason channel_disabled (nothing is retried, nothing fails):

for pair in api_key=<ApiKey> secret_key=<SecretKey> brandname=<Brandname>; do
  curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/kernel/settings/esms.connection.sms.${pair%%=*}" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d "{\"value\": \"${pair#*=}\"}"
done

SePay eInvoice — e-invoices, and an invoice series that uses it:

for pair in client_id=<client id> client_secret=<client secret> provider_account_id=<provider account id>; do
  curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/kernel/settings/einvoice_sepay.connection.api.${pair%%=*}" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d "{\"value\": \"${pair#*=}\"}"
done
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/invoices/series" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"kind": "vat_invoice", "symbol": "1C26TAA", "year": 2026, "einvoice_provider": "sepay"}'

GHN — delivery. The carrier account answers with webhook_path once: keep it. Then a delivery method that uses the account and books each parcel with GHN by itself (auto_book):

GHN=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/shipping/carrier-accounts" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"carrier_code\": \"ghn\", \"name\": \"GHN\", \"credentials\": {\"token\": \"<GHN token>\"}, \"settings\": {\"shop_id\": <ShopId>}}")
echo "$GHN" | jq -r .data.webhook_path
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/shipping/methods" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"code\": \"ghn\", \"kind\": \"carrier\", \"names\": {\"vi\": \"Giao Hàng Nhanh\", \"en\": \"GHN\"}, \"currency\": \"VND\", \"carrier_account_id\": \"$(echo "$GHN" | jq -r .data.id)\", \"auto_book\": true}"

No e-invoice account yet? A series without einvoice_provider still numbers your invoices inside Autoxide (internal documents, not sent to the tax authority). Without any series no invoice is issued at delivery; with two series of the same kind, year and unit, pick one when issuing (series_id). Ask for an order’s invoice again with POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/invoice.

curl -sS "$API/api/v1/invoices/series" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"kind": "vat_invoice", "symbol": "1C26TAA", "year": 2026}'

Tax profile — who sells and under which tax rules. Tax on the cart and the seller on every invoice (name, tax code, address) come from it; without it tax is computed as vat_credit and invoices carry no seller details. regime is vat_credit, vat_direct, household or exempt; business_lines are goods, services, production_transport or other:

curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/tax/profiles/$ORG" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jurisdiction": "VN", "regime": "vat_credit", "tax_code": "0101234567", "legal_name": "Công ty TNHH Trà Việt",
       "business_lines": ["goods"]}'

Your own delivery fees (no carrier account): a zone, a method with fixed rates by weight. A zone listing a province ({"country": "VN", "province_code": "01"}) wins over the whole country. Items without a weight use default_item_weight_grams:

ZONE=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/shipping/zones" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"name\": \"Viet Nam\", \"regions\": [{\"country\": \"VN\"}]}" | jq -r .data.id)
METHOD=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/shipping/methods" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"code\": \"standard\", \"kind\": \"table_rate\", \"names\": {\"vi\": \"Giao tiêu chuẩn\", \"en\": \"Standard\"},
       \"currency\": \"VND\", \"default_item_weight_grams\": 500}" | jq -r .data.id)
curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/shipping/methods/$METHOD/rates" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"rates\": [{\"zone_id\": \"$ZONE\", \"min_weight_grams\": 0, \"amount\": {\"amount\": \"30000\", \"currency\": \"VND\"}}]}"

In the GHN dashboard, register AUTOXIDE_HTTP_PUBLIC_URL + webhook_path as the status webhook (see the GHN guide). MoMo needs nothing: Autoxide sends your notification address with every payment.

3. A product with a price and stock

Give the weight and size of one item: GHN prices the parcel from them.

PRODUCT=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/catalog/products" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"kind": "physical", "tax_category": "vat.standard", "locale": "vi", "text": {"name": "Ấm trà"},
       "variant": {"sku": "TEA-1", "weight_grams": 600, "length_mm": 300, "width_mm": 200, "height_mm": 100}}')
PRODUCT_ID=$(echo "$PRODUCT" | jq -r .data.id)
VARIANT=$(echo "$PRODUCT" | jq -r '.data.variants[0].id')

LIST=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/catalog/price-lists" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name": "Retail", "currency": "VND", "tax_inclusive": true}' | jq -r .data.id)
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/catalog/price-lists/$LIST/prices" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"reference\": \"opening\", \"prices\": [{\"variant_id\": \"$VARIANT\", \"amount\": {\"amount\": \"150000\", \"currency\": \"VND\"}}]}"

curl -sS "$API/api/v1/inventory/adjustments" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"org_id\": \"$ORG\", \"reference\": \"opening-1\", \"lines\": [{\"variant_id\": \"$VARIANT\", \"quantity\": 10, \"reason_code\": \"found\", \"unit_cost\": {\"amount\": \"90000\", \"currency\": \"VND\"}}]}"

curl -sS -X POST "$API/api/v1/catalog/products/$PRODUCT_ID/status" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'If-Match: *' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"status": "active"}'

4. Buy it as a guest

This is what your website does. No sign-in: the cart is identified by the Cart-Token it returns. Prices, discounts, tax and delivery fee are always computed by the server.

CART=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/carts" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{}')
CART_ID=$(echo "$CART" | jq -r .data.id)
CART_TOKEN=$(echo "$CART" | jq -r .data.cart_token)

curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/orders/carts/$CART_ID/lines/$VARIANT" -H "Cart-Token: $CART_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"quantity": 2}'

# Where to deliver: codes from GET /api/v1/address/provinces and GET /api/v1/address/units?province=<code>.
curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/orders/carts/$CART_ID/delivery" -H "Cart-Token: $CART_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"recipient_name": "Nguyễn An", "phone": "0912345678",
       "address": {"country": "VN", "province_code": "01", "commune_code": "00004", "line": "12 Tràng Tiền"}}' \
  | jq '.data.shipping.options'

The options list GHN’s fee for this parcel (the recipient, the address and the parcel’s weight and size are sent to GHN). Choose one by sending its option_key with the same address, then place the order. Keep the Idempotency-Key: sending the same request again returns the same order.

QUOTE=$(curl -sS -X PUT "$API/api/v1/orders/carts/$CART_ID/delivery" -H "Cart-Token: $CART_TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"recipient_name": "Nguyễn An", "phone": "0912345678", "shipping_option": "<option_key>",
       "address": {"country": "VN", "province_code": "01", "commune_code": "00004", "line": "12 Tràng Tiền"}}' \
  | jq -r .data.quote_token)

curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/checkout" -H "Cart-Token: $CART_TOKEN" -H 'Idempotency-Key: first-order-1' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d "{\"cart_id\": \"$CART_ID\", \"quote_token\": \"$QUOTE\", \"payment_method\": \"wallet\",
       \"return_url\": \"https://shop.example.com/thank-you\",
       \"contact\": {\"name\": \"Nguyễn An\", \"phone\": \"0912345678\"}}"

return_url is the page of your website the buyer comes back to after paying (on one of your shop’s addresses, see step 2); MoMo requires one (or set momo.connection.account.return_url once for your shop). The answer has the order (pending_payment) and payment_actions[0]: a redirect to MoMo’s payment page. When the buyer has paid, MoMo notifies your server, and the order becomes confirmed / paid on its own. The buyer receives an SMS for the order placed and for the payment confirmed.

payment_method is cod (cash on delivery, when the cart has a delivery), bank_transfer, or a method of the payment partner (wallet, card…); the cart’s payment_methods lists cod, bank_transfer and gateway, where gateway stands for any method of the partner. Checkout gives a guest no token to read the order later: the buyer asks for one with the order number and phone, and confirms the code sent by SMS. The access_token then goes in the Order-Token header of /api/v1/orders/public/orders/… calls:

LOOKUP=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/public/orders/lookup" -H 'Content-Type: application/json'   -d '{"number": "<order number>", "phone": "0912345678"}' | jq -r .data.lookup_id)
ORDER_TOKEN=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/public/orders/lookup/$LOOKUP/confirm" -H 'Content-Type: application/json'   -d '{"code": "<code from the SMS>"}' | jq -r .data.access_token)

5. Ship it

Note the order id from the checkout answer. Requesting the shipment accepts the order (preparing); GHN books the parcel by itself because the method has auto_book:

ORDER=<order id>
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/$ORDER/shipments" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{}'
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/shipping/shipments?order_id=$ORDER" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"

From then on GHN’s status webhook moves the order: picked up → shipping, delivered → delivered. At delivery the e-invoice is issued through SePay eInvoice (orders.invoice_timing is on_delivered by default) and the buyer receives an SMS.

curl -sS "$API/api/v1/orders/$ORDER" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" | jq '.data | {status, payment_status}'
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/invoices/documents?source_module=orders&source_id=$ORDER" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  | jq '.data[0] | {status, number, authority_code}'

Try it without real accounts

Before you have provider accounts, run the whole flow in a sandbox: a test copy of a shop where every partner is simulated (payments, delivery, SMS, e-invoices) and nothing reaches a real one. A sandbox needs a server with several tenants, and its payments are completed by hand, so allow that too (both settings are off by default):

# Next to compose.quickstart.yml: several tenants, and simulated payments allowed inside sandboxes.
cat > compose.try.yml <<'EOF'
services:
  autoxide:
    environment:
      AUTOXIDE_TENANCY_MODE: multi
      AUTOXIDE_LEDGER_SANDBOX_SIMULATION: "true"
EOF
docker compose -f compose.quickstart.yml -f compose.try.yml up -d

# A shop tenant and its first administrator (the command prints the password).
X="docker compose -f compose.quickstart.yml -f compose.try.yml exec autoxide autoxide"
$X tenant create --slug demo --name "Demo shop"
$X user create --tenant demo --email owner@demo.example --name "Owner" --admin

# Sign in to the shop, then create a sandbox of it.
API=http://127.0.0.1:8080
TOKEN=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/auth/login" -H 'Autoxide-Tenant: demo' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"email":"owner@demo.example","password":"<printed password>"}' | jq -r .data.access_token)
TENANT=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/kernel/tenant" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Autoxide-Tenant: demo' | jq -r .data.id)
SANDBOX=$(curl -sS "$API/api/v1/kernel/tenants/$TENANT/sandboxes" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H 'Autoxide-Tenant: demo' -H "Idempotency-Key: $(openssl rand -hex 16)" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name": "First sale"}' | jq -r .data.id)
curl -sS "$API/api/v1/kernel/tenant" -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H "Autoxide-Tenant: $SANDBOX" | jq .data.status

The status is active once the copy is done (a few seconds). Then do steps 1 to 5 with two changes:

  • Send -H "Autoxide-Tenant: $SANDBOX" with every call, the public ones too (cart, checkout, address codes): without a sign-in, that header is what picks the shop. Skip step 2’s provider accounts; in step 1 AUTOXIDE_MODULES still names the connectors, so their simulated twins exist.
  • A new sandbox is empty: it copies settings, roles and pages, not products, prices, stock, delivery methods, the tax profile or invoice series. Create them inside it (step 2’s series without einvoice_provider, the tax profile, your own delivery fees; step 3). If there are several payment partners, set the default one inside the sandbox too.

After checkout the payment stays pending, because no partner notifies a sandbox. Complete it as the partner would: see Complete a simulated payment. The order then becomes confirmed / paid, and you continue with step 5. Delete the sandbox when you are done (DELETE /api/v1/kernel/sandboxes/{id} from the shop, without Autoxide-Tenant: $SANDBOX).

When something does not happen

What you seeWhy, and what to do
A call answers provider.not_configuredA field of that provider account is not set. GET /api/v1/kernel/settings?group=connection lists what each connection needs.
An SMS has status skipped, reason channel_disabled (GET /api/v1/notifications/messages)eSMS is not set up for your shop yet. Messages before that are not sent again.
Checkout answers that provider is requiredThe server has several payment partners. Set your default one: PUT /api/v1/kernel/settings/ledger.payments.default_provider with {"value": "momo"}.
payment_actions[0].kind is noneThe payment could not be started at MoMo; payment_actions[0].error says why (code and message, e.g. momo.return_url_required). Fix it, then start the payment again: POST /api/v1/orders/{id}/payment-link (shop staff), POST /api/v1/orders/me/orders/{id}/payment-link (a signed-in buyer) or POST /api/v1/orders/public/orders/{id}/payment-link with the Order-Token header (a guest buyer); the buyer may send {"return_url": "…"}.
payment_actions[0].error.code is orders.return_url_not_allowed (or payment-link answers 422 with that code)return_url is not on one of your shop’s addresses. Add its origin to ledger.payments.return_origins (step 2) or verify the domain, then start the payment again.
GET /api/v1/ledger/payments/{id} answers 404 for a payment_idpayment_actions[].payment_id is the order’s own payment. Use payment_actions[].ledger_payment_id with the ledger endpoints.
payment-link returns a new payment_idThe partner reported the previous collection failed (the buyer declined or gave up), so a new attempt was started (status superseded on the old one). Show the buyer the new action and use its status_token. If the partner still collects the old one, that money is refunded through the partner on its own.
A payment is needs_review and the order stays pending_paymentThe partner reported another amount than the amount due. A corrected notification for the same transaction does not clear it: someone with ledger.reconcile finds the amount_mismatch item (GET /api/v1/ledger/exceptions?status=open) and resolves it with POST /api/v1/ledger/exceptions/{id}/resolve: {"action": "accept_received", "payment_id": "<ledger payment id>"} records the amount collected and confirms the order if it is still waiting for payment. Resolve it before the order’s payment deadline (30 minutes by default, AUTOXIDE_LEDGER_PAYMENT_EXPIRY_MINUTES): after it, the order is already cancelled, so the amount collected is refunded through the partner on its own and the order is not reopened. refunded_outside or ignore close the item and the order is cancelled at its deadline.
A late_payment item is open in GET /api/v1/ledger/exceptionsMoney arrived after the payment’s deadline or after the partner reported it failed. The order decides: if it still counts the money (the order goes on), the item closes on its own (resolution kept) within moments; when the order no longer needs it, Autoxide refunds it through the partner on its own, and the item closes once the payment is refunded in full (resolution refunded). Never refund an item whose order went on. An item still open means the partner refused that refund: first check refunds in GET /api/v1/ledger/payments/{id} (a refund may still be pending at the partner), and only then refund outside Autoxide and resolve the item with refunded_outside, so the buyer is not refunded twice.
A refund_failed item is open in GET /api/v1/ledger/exceptionsThe partner refused a refund of that payment, or the refund could not be sent at all (for example a bank transfer, which no partner refunds, of an order cancelled after the money arrived). Nothing is sent again on its own. Refund the buyer outside Autoxide, then resolve the item with refunded_outside; if a later refund through the partner returns the whole payment, the item closes on its own. A refund answered with 409 or 429, or with a server error, was not refused: it stays pending and is sent again with the same reference.
The order stays pending_paymentMoMo could not reach AUTOXIDE_HTTP_PUBLIC_URL. Check the address and look at GET /api/v1/kernel/settings?group=connection for the last error.
GHN’s option is missing from the cartGHN refused the quote. The carrier account shows the last error (GET /api/v1/shipping/carrier-accounts); check the item’s weight and the address.
The order does not move after GHN picked the parcelThe GHN webhook is not registered, or GHN has not applied it yet (about 15 minutes).
An ETag / If-Match errorRead the record again and send its current ETag (If-Match: * skips the check).
